# No Fail Chocolate Cake with Fudge Frosting

**Contributor:** Cheryl Reider

**Getty Entity:** Trust

**Department:** Human Resources

---

## Servings & Ingredients

Cake: 2 cups sugar 2/3 cup shortening 4 extra large eggs 2 squares bakers chocolate 2 cups buttermilk 2 teaspoons baking soda 2 1/2 cups all-purpose flour 1/2 teaspoon salt 1 teaspoon vanilla Fudge frosting: 1 square bakers chocolate 1/4 cup butter 1/3 cup milk pinch of salt

---

## Instructions

Preheat oven to 350Â° F. Melt the chocolate in the oven. Blend sugar and shortening together until creamy, then add the eggs, then the melted chocolate (slightly cooled) and finally the vanilla. Stir baking soda into the buttermilk. Continue to stir until you hear the pitch change. Sift flour and salt together then begin to add the milk and flour alternately to the sugar, shortening mix in three rounds. Pour into greased and lightly powdered (with confectioners' sugar) large rectangular baking pan (9x12x2 is ok). Bake for 40 - 50 minutes until it passes the toothpick test. To make the frosting, melt chocolate and butter over hot water (double boiler). Add other ingredients. Boil for 3 minutes. Do not stir at all. Take off heat and beat for 10 minutes and promptly spread.

## About This Recipe

This recipe has been handed down in Cheryl's family from generation to generation. Over time, most "dashes and pinches" have been replaced with everyday measurements. Cheryl's father's mother was an exceptional fudge and English toffee maker and she handed down the recipe by way of family involvement. This was their traditional birthday and special occasion cake. All the children took part in making this cake listening for the pitch to change in the buttermilk as they stirred and would anxiously wait to see who would receive the honor of beating the fudge, because the lucky child got to lick the spoon! (But only if they were careful not to beat it too soon, turning the mixture to sugar, in which case they would have to start all over again...) Cheryl's department's food tradition is chocolate. What a wonderful mood enhancer it is! Used in a strictly medicinal manner, they are never in short supply.
