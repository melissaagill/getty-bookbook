# Apple Crisp

**Contributor:** Sally McKay

**Getty Entity:** Research Institute

**Department:** Library

---

## Servings & Ingredients

Serves 6 5 cups green apples (Pippin are best) 1 teaspoon water 1 teaspoon ground cinnamon 1/2 teaspoon salt Topping: 3/4 cup granulated sugar 3/4 cup all-purpose flour 1/3 cup softened butter or margarine

---

## Instructions

Preheat oven to 375Â° F. Peel, pare and cut apples into pieces. Mix the apples with water, cinnamon and salt. Place the apple mixture into an 8" x 8" x 2" pan or casserole.  To create the topping, mix together the flour, butter and sugar until crumbly and pour over the apples. Bake uncovered for about 40 minutes. Serve warm with ice cream.
