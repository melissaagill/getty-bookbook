# "Crumb" Apple Pie

**Contributor:** Katherine Hyman

**Getty Entity:** Research Institute

**Department:** Technical Services

---

## Servings & Ingredients

Serves 8 6 Golden Delicious large apples 1 cup sugar 1/3 cup butter or margarine, melted 3/4 cup all-purpose flour 1 teaspoon ground cinnamon Store-bought pastry for one 9" piecrust

---

## Instructions

Prepare pastry according to box directions and put into a 9" glass pie pan. Peel, core and slice apples very thin. Divide sugar into two 1/2 cups. Stir 1/2 cup sugar with the cinnamon and sprinkle a little of this mixture on bottom of pie crust. Lay first layer of apples and sprinkle apples with same mixture. Repeat until all apples are in the pan. Mix remaining 1/2 cup sugar with flour and add melted butter or margarine. Form thick crumbs with fingers and cover evenly over apples slowly with fingers. Bake for 10 min. in a 450Â° F oven then reduce to 350Â° F and bake for 30 minutes more, constantly checking until pie is golden brown.

## About This Recipe

This apple pie won Katherine Hyman the first Beverly Hills Market "Apple Pie" contest in October of 1995. Judged by two apple growers and the Mayor and Vice-Mayor of Beverly Hills, the pie was presented on a tray covered with a small American flag. The Beverly Hills News weekly wrote an article, titled "It's all in the Crumbs."
