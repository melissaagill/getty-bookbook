# Lemon Squares

**Contributor:** Jennifer Daft

**Getty Entity:** Trust

**Department:** Administration

---

## Servings & Ingredients

Crust: 1 1/2 cups all-purpose flour 1/2 cup confectioners' sugar 6 ozs. cold sweet butter (1 1/2 sticks) Filling: 1 cup plus 2 tablespoons freshly squeezed lemon juice 6 extra large eggs 3 cups sugar 1/2 cups flour 1/4 cup confectioners' sugar (for dusting)

---

## Instructions

Preheat oven to 325Â° F. Crust: Combine flour and confectioners' sugar in bowl. Add butter and blend with an electric mixer on low speed until crumbs of mixture are the size of small peas. Press the crust into the bottom of a 9 x 13" pan. Bake for 20-25 minutes or until golden brown. Remove from oven. Decrease oven to 300Â° F. To make filling, whisk together eggs and sugar in large bowl until smooth. Stir in lemon juice and then flour. Pour this mixture into crust and bake 40 minutes until lemon filling is set. Cool for 30 minutes, slice into squares and dust with confectioners' sugar.
