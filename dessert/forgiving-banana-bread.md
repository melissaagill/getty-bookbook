# Forgiving Banana Bread

**Contributor:** Rheeah Yoo

**Getty Entity:** Research Institute

**Department:** Technical Services

---

## Servings & Ingredients

Yields one large loaf 1 1/2 cups sugar 1 1/2 cups butter 1 cup bananas, mashed (2 large bananas) 1 cup nuts, chopped 3 extra large eggs, separated 2 1/2 cups all-purpose flour 1/2 teaspoon baking soda 1 teaspoon salt 1 teaspoon vanilla

---

## Instructions

Separate 3 egg yolks from whites. In large bowl beat egg whites until stiff peaks are formed. Set aside in cool place. In large bowl cream sugar and butter. Mix in 1 cup mashed bananas and nuts. Add and thoroughly mix egg yolks, flour, baking soda, salt, and vanilla. Fold in egg whites. Bake at 350Â° F for one hour. Notes: More butter makes loaf moister. Walnut crumbs sprinkled on top before baking enhance the loaf's beauty. Mixture can be divided into smaller portions to fill 2 or 3 smaller loaf molds. 1/2 cup wheat flour substitution and 1/2 cup sugar reduction makes heartier bread. Bread can also be baked in a pan, coffee-cake style.
