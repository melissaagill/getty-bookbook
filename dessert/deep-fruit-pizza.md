# Deep Fruit Pizza

**Contributor:** Ellen South

**Getty Entity:** Museum

**Department:** Sculpture, Curatorial

---

## Servings & Ingredients

Serves 8 Piecrust: 1 package (1/3 lb) whole wheat graham crackers 2 1/2 tablespoons maple syrup 2 tablespoons sesame tahini 1-2 teaspoons water Pizza: 1 lb. firm tofu 1 teaspoon vanilla extract finely grated zest of 2 organic lemons 1/4 cup sugar 2 cups fresh fruit, sliced (strawberries, peaches, kiwis, grapes, blueberries...) 1/4 cup orange marmalade 1 teaspoon fresh lemon juice

---

## Instructions

Preheat oven to 350Â° F. To make crust: Pulverize the graham crackers in a food processor. Add the remaining ingredients and process until well blended. Press mixture evenly into a 9" pie pan for a deeper fruit pizza or a 14" pizza pan and bake for 20 mins. Remove from heat, cool, and place in the freezer to chill. Drain tofu and process in food processor until smooth, adding vanilla extract, lemon zest and sugar. Spread the mixture on top of chilled crust and refrigerate for at least an hour. Arrange the sliced fruit on top of the tofu mixture. In a small saucepan heat the marmalade and lemon juice until bubbly. (This can be done in a small glass or ceramic bowl in a microwave.) Drizzle the mixture over the pizza and chill 1 hour more.

## About This Recipe

Ellen South lives in Topanga and likes food to be sensual, organic, simple and yummy. This recipe is from the book "Eating Well for Optimum Health" by Andrew Weil, and can be either a fruit pizza as he describes, or a deeper pizza pie. It can be eaten absolutely any time and the ingredients can be adjusted accordingly. With exotic additions for evening. Like spiked cream whip.
