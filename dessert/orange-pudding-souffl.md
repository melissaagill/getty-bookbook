## Servings & Ingredients

Serves 6 1 1/4 cups sugar 3 tablespoons all-purpose flour large pinch of salt 1 cup whole milk 4 extra large eggs, separated 1/2 cup fresh orange juice 1/4 cup fresh lemon juice 1/4 cup (1/2 stick) unsalted butter, melted and cooled 1 1/2 tablespoons orange zest 1 1/2 teaspoons lemon zest 1/2 teaspoon vanilla extract 3 quarter-size rounds crystallized ginger, thinly sliced fresh raspberries (optional) fresh mint (optional)

---

## Instructions

Preheat oven to 350Â° F. Arrange six 1 1/4-cup soufflÃ© dishes or custard cups in large roasting pan, spacing them apart. Stir 1 cup sugar, flour and salt in large bowl and blend. Whisk in milk, egg yolks, fruit juices, butter, orange zest, lemon zest and vanilla extract (batter will be thin). Using electric mixer, beat whites in medium-sized bowl until very frothy. Gradually add remaining 1/4 cup sugar, beating until medium-firm peaks form. Fold 1/4 of whites into batter to lighten. Fold in remaining whites. Divide batter among soufflÃ© dishes. Top with ginger slices. Pour hot water in roasting pan until halfway up sides of dishes. Bake cakes until tops are golden brown and beginning to split and pudding layer on bottom appears thickened, about 40 minutes. Transfer desserts to rack; cool at least 20 minutes. Serve warm or at room temperature. Can be made 1 day ahead. Cover and refrigerate; serve cold. Garnish cakes with fresh raspberries and mint if desired, and serve.
