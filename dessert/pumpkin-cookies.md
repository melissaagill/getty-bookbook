## Instructions

Preheat oven to 350Â° F. Combine flour, baking soda, baking powder, cinnamon, nutmeg, salt, raisins and nuts in medium bowl. Cream butter and sugar in large mixing bowl. Add pumpkin, egg and vanilla; beat until light and creamy. Mix in dry ingredients slowly until well blended. Drop by rounded tablespoon onto greased cookie sheet. Bake 15 to 20 minutes. Cool on wire racks.  Glaze: Blend confectioners' sugar, milk, melted butter and vanilla extract in small bowl until smooth. Drizzle glaze over tops of cooled cookies.
