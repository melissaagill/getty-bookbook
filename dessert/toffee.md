# Toffee

**Contributor:** Marty Jo Harkness

**Getty Entity:** Trust

**Department:** President's Office

---

## Servings & Ingredients

1 candy thermometer Toffee: 1 lb. butter 2 cups sugar 1/2 cup water 2 tablespoons white corn syrup Topping: about 1 bar Baker's  semi-sweet chocolate about 1 cup almonds, finely chopped

---

## Instructions

In a large (4-quart) heavy saucepan, mix in all toffee ingredients. Place saucepan on high heat. Pull up a chair, have a oven mitt handy for stirring over heat, and stir constantly. It will boil, just keep stirring until candy thermometer reaches 290F. Immediately pour onto ungreased cookie sheet (with sides). No need to spread the mixture, it will level out and harden very quickly. Rinse saucepan, stirring utensil, thermometer right away or clean-up will be difficult. While waiting for toffee to cool. Chop almonds, set aside. Melt chocolate in saucepan. After it is melted, let it cool slightly to avoid cracking toffee on contact. Spread over toffee. Sprinkle almonds over chocolate. Change amounts of chocolate or almonds to taste. Let it sit for a few hours or overnight. Score with knife and cut into square a la Martha Stewart or break into pieces with any utensil a la Mrs. Murphy, the recipe's originator.
