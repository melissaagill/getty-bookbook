# Almond Torte

**Contributor:** Lynn LaBate

**Getty Entity:** Museum

**Department:** Education

---

## Servings & Ingredients

Serves 8-10 1/2 cup butter, softened 1 cup sugar 1 7-ozs. tube almond paste, cut into small pieces 3 extra large eggs 1/4 teaspoon Amaretto 1/2 teaspoon Triple Sec 1 teaspoon baking powder 1 cup all-purpose flour confectioners' sugar

---

## Instructions

Cream the butter and sugar by beating with an electric mixer until fluffy, 1 to 2 minutes. Gradually add almond paste, beat until incorporated. Add the eggs, one at a time, beat well between each addition. Then beat the Amaretto and the Triple Sec into the mixture, next fold in the flour and baking powder. Pour the batter into a greased and floured 8" round cake pan and bake until lightly brown, 35 to 45 minutes. Let cool slightly in the pan and remove. Invert the cake on to a cake rack and let cool completely and dust with confectioners' sugar.
