# Ginger Creams

**Contributor:** Brenda Smith

**Getty Entity:** Museum

**Department:** Information and Media Systems

---

## Servings & Ingredients

Yields 6 dozen cookies 1 cup sugar 1 cup butter 1 cup molasses 1 cup boiling water 3 teaspoons baking soda 1 extra large egg, beaten 1 tablespoon ground ginger 6 cups (approximately) all-purpose flour

---

## Instructions

Mix ingredients together, adding flour last. (Add enough flour to make dough drop.) Drop by teaspoonfuls onto baking sheet. Bake at 350Â° F for 8-12 minutes. Dip in confectioners' sugar while warm.

## About This Recipe

Brenda and her husband Mike work in the Digital Imaging Lab at the Getty Museum. A longtime favorite at Christmastime for the Smith family, this cookie recipe came from Mike's grandmother. Mike sometimes uses this cookie dough to make cut-out cookies, frosting them after they are baked. Brenda prefers to make them the easy way, as described here.
