# Kathryn Tyner's "Bestest" Cookies

**Contributor:** Nancy Tyner

**Getty Entity:** Research Institute

**Department:** Library

---

## Servings & Ingredients

Yields 2-3 dozen 2 cups sifted all-purpose flour 1/2 teaspoon salt 2 teaspoon baking powder 1 teaspoon vanilla 2/3 cup butter 2/3 cup sugar 2 extra large eggs

---

## Instructions

Cream butter and sugar together. Next add eggs and vanilla then beat until smooth and well blended. Stir in salt, baking powder, and some of the flour until well blended. Gradually stir in the remaining flour until the dough is stiff but not dry. Cover dough and let it chill in the refrigerator for 2 hours (you can keep the dough in the refrigerator overnight, but you will need to wrap it in plastic wrap first). Preheat the oven to 375Â° F. Roll the dough to about 1/4 inch thick. Cut dough with a cookie cutter and place on a greased cookie sheet. Bake for about 10-12 minutes, depending on the size of the cookie. Cool on a rack. To decorate: Frost with your favorite buttercream frosting and top with sprinkles ... use your imagination! If you only want to decorate with sugar, very lightly brush the top of the cookie with water then sprinkle with sugar before baking.

## About This Recipe

Nancy's mother, Kathryn, who is a Serials Assistant at the Research Library, uses this basic recipe to create decorated sugar cookies for any holiday. Throughout the years she has helped her children and then grandchildren cut the dough with cookie cutters and decorate the cookies. She made these cookies many times as a room mother. Years ago, during a valentine party, a third grade classmate of Nancy's declared these cookies the "bestest". Nancy and Kathryn have all kinds of cookie cutters and decorating items, and most of all, many wonderful memories.
