# Citrus Flan

**Contributor:** Hripsime Krikorian

**Getty Entity:** Museum

**Department:** Bookstore

---

## Servings & Ingredients

Serves 8-10 9 extra large eggs 7 cups milk 2 1/2 cups sugar 1/4 teaspoon lemon juice 1 tablespoon water 1 teaspoon vanilla extract 1/2 teaspoon lemon zest 1/2 teaspoon orange zest 1 tablespoon Grand Marnier 2 tablespoons orange juice (optional)

---

## Instructions

Preheat oven to 350Â° F. In a sauce pan, combine 1 1/2 cups sugar, water, and lemon juice. Heat over a medium flame until it reaches a caramel color. Pour immediately in a 10 x 14 Pyrex baking dish. In a separate large bowl, mix the remaining ingredients with a hand whisk, pouring over caramel mixture. Place Pyrex baker in a larger baking dish then fill outer dish with hot water. Bake for 1 hour. Let cool on a rack at room temperature then chill in refrigerator for at least six hours. Delicious served with additional Grand Mariner sprinkled on top. Anoosh ullah!

## About This Recipe

This is one of many recipes given to Hripsime by his mother's Uncle Nicholas, a cook and musician who never fully realized his dreams of becoming a professional musician in his native country of Karabagh. Instead, he excelled in becoming a chef while restoring old musical instruments and playing the mandolin and twelve-string guitars in his free time.
