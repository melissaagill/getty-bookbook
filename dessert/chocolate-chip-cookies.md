# Chocolate Chip Cookies

**Contributor:** Valerie Gross

**Getty Entity:** Trust

**Department:** Administration

---

## Servings & Ingredients

Yields 2 dozen cookies 1/4 lb. butter (1 stick) 1/2 cup dark brown sugar 1/2 cup sugar 1 egg 3/4 teaspoon vanilla 1/2 teaspoon baking soda 1/2 teaspoon salt 1 1/8 cups flour 1 cup (6oz.) chocolate chips 1/2 cup oatmeal

---

## Instructions

Heat oven to 375Â° F. Grease cookie sheets. Cream butter until fluffy then cream in sugars until well blended. Beat egg into creamed mixture and combine with vanilla. In a separate bowl combine flour, salt and baking soda; mix well. Add dry ingredients to creamed mixture and blend. Drop a teaspoon at a time on cookie sheets, allowing an inch between each cookie. Bake for 8-10 minutes. Cool on a rack.
