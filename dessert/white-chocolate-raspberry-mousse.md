# White Chocolate Raspberry Mousse

**Contributor:** Bruce Metro

**Getty Entity:** Museum

**Department:** Preparation

---

## Servings & Ingredients

Serves 6 8 ozs. white chocolate 1/2 vanilla bean 3 extra large egg whites (pasteurized) 1/3 cup superfine granulated sugar 2 cups heavy whipping cream 6 ozs. fresh raspberries

---

## Instructions

The steps must all be done quickly. Production time is about 15-20 minutes. In a chilled bowl, large enough to hold both the whipped egg whites and the whipped cream, whip the egg whites until stiff and glossy. While continuing to whip eggs, add superfine sugar in small amounts. Place in refrigerator. In a chilled bowl whip cream until very stiff. While whipping the cream, melt white chocolate in a double boiler. Do not overheat chocolate. It must be just hot enough to flow, slowly. If too hot, let cool. As chocolate melts, slit vanilla bean, scrape seeds and stir them into melted chocolate. Retrieve whipped egg whites and, using a wide spatula, carefully fold the whipped cream into the egg whites. Add melted chocolate with a large spoon; drizzling in the chocolate while continuing to carefully fold the mixture. The chocolate must be melted enough to flow but not so hot that it runs. As you complete adding the chocolate, grab a handful of raspberries and, holding them over the bowl, squeeze your hand, squashing the berries. Drop the smashed berries into the bowl carefully folding them in. Repeat until all berries are used. Fold the berries in as little, definitive swirls and color contrast, or as much, equal distribution and uniform color, as you like, but do not over stir. Quickly spoon mixture into chilled individual pot de crÃ¨me cups, ramekins, or similar small bowls. Clear glass is also nice, try a martini glass. Chill in refrigerator for 3 to 4 hours. Garnish with whipped cream, mint leaves, whole raspberries, rough grated or solid piece of chocolate.
