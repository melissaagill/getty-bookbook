# Apple Cake

**Contributor:** Mark Henderson

**Getty Entity:** Research Institute

**Department:** Library

---

## Servings & Ingredients

Serves 16-18 1 1/2 cups salad oil 2 extra large eggs 3 cups all-purpose flour, sifted 1 teaspoon salt 1 teaspoon ground cinnamon 1 cup pecans, chopped 2 cups sugar 2 teaspoons vanilla 1 teaspoon soda 1 teaspoon ground nutmeg 1 can sliced pie apples confectioners' sugar (optional)

---

## Instructions

Combine and mix well salad oil, sugar, eggs, and vanilla. Sift together flour, soda, salt, nutmeg, and cinnamon; add to above mixture. Stir apples and pecans into combined mixture. Bake at 325Â° F. in a greased Bundt pan for 70 to 75 minutes. Remove from oven and cool on a rack. Dust the top with confectioners' sugar (optional).
