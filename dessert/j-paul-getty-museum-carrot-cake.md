# J. Paul Getty Museum Carrot Cake

**Contributor:** Yvonne Szafran

**Getty Entity:** Museum

**Department:** Paintings Conservation

---

## Servings & Ingredients

1 1/2 cups corn oil 2 cups sugar 3 extra large eggs 2 cups all-purpose flour 2 teaspoons ground cinnamon 2 teaspoons soda 2 teaspoons vanilla 1 teaspoon salt 2 cups carrots, shredded 1 cup walnuts, chopped 1/2 cup crushed pineapple, drained Cream Cheese Frosting: 1 8-oz. package cream cheese 1 1/2 cups confectioners' sugar 1/2 cup margarine 1/8 cup crushed pineapple 1/4 cup walnuts, chopped

---

## Instructions

Cake: Combine oil, sugar, eggs, flour, cinnamon, soda, vanilla, salt carrots, walnuts and pineapple in a large bowl. Mix until blended. Pour into a greased 13 x 9" pan and bake at 350Â° F until done, approximately 50-60 minutes. Set aside to cool on a rack. To make cream cheese frosting, mix cream cheese, sugar and margarine until fluffy; add pineapple and walnuts. Mix well. Ice cake with Cream Cheese Frosting when cool.


---

## About This Recipe

Yvonne Szafran is Associate Conservator of Paintings for the Museum. She has worked at the Getty for over 20 years. She remembers that when the Villa first opened to the public in 1974, an item which was immensely popular with many visitors was the delicious carrot cake served in the cafe. For many people it became a significant memory, in addition to the art, building and gardens, of their Getty visit.
