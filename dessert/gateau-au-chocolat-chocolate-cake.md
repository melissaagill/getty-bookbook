# Gateau au Chocolat (Chocolate Cake)

**Contributor:** Ashley Tierney

**Getty Entity:** Trust

**Department:** nan

---

## Servings & Ingredients

Serves 8-12 12 ozs. bittersweet chocolate, broken into pieces 2/3 cup unsalted butter 3/4 cup granulated sugar 5 extra large eggs, separated 1/3 cup unbleached all-purpose flour about 2 teaspoons confectioners' sugar, for decoration (optional)

---

## Instructions

Preheat the oven to 350Â° F. Butter a 9 1/2-inch springform pan or deep nonstick cake pan. Combine chocolate, butter, and granulated sugar in the top of a double boiler placed over simmering water. Melt over medium heat, stirring until the ingredients are thoroughly blended. Set the mixture aside to cool. Whisk the egg yolks into the chocolate. Whisk in the flour. Beat the egg whites in a large bowl until they form firm peaks; do not overbeat. Add one-third of the egg whites to the chocolate batter and mix vigorously. Gently fold in the remaining whites. Do this slowly and patiently. Do not overmix, but be sure that the mixture is well blended and that no streaks of whites remain. Pour batter into the prepared pan. Bake until the cake is firm and springy, 35 to 40 minutes. Cool on a rack for several hours before unmolding. The cake is traditionally served without icing (though you may want to dust it with the confectioners' sugar).

## About This Recipe

This is a simple but rich chocolate cake from one of Ashley's favorite French bistro cookbooks, and it is easy to make. The key is to use good chocolate (Lindt, Tobler, or Valrhona, for example) and good butter, and underbake the cake a little bit so that it's slightly gooey in the middle. Serve it plain with a bit of confectioners' sugar, or dress it up with raspberries and chocolate sauce or caramel sauce.
