# Crustless Pumpkin Pie

**Contributor:** Randy Osherow

**Getty Entity:** Trust

**Department:** President's Office

---

## Servings & Ingredients

Serves 10-14 Filling: 1 28-oz. can pumpkin pack (not pumpkin pie mix with seasonings included) 1 can evaporated milk 1 cup brown sugar 3 extra large eggs, beaten 1 tablespoon ground cinnamon 2 teaspoons ground nutmeg 1 teaspoon ground cloves 1 teaspoon ground ginger Note: The seasoning measurements can be increased or decreased to taste. Topping: 1 box yellow cake mix (a mix without pudding) 1 1/2 cubes of melted butter or margarine 1 cup pecans, chopped

---

## Instructions

Filling: In a large bowl mix the ingredients and pour into greased 9 x 13" pan or glass dish (Glass tends to cook more evenly). Topping: Sprinkle cake mix evenly over wet mixture (do not worry about lumpy surface; this makes for a more crispy texture). Drizzle melted butter over cake mix topping. Sprinkle on pecans to complete. Bake for 50 minutes at 350Â° F or until topping is golden brown. Cool and serve. Wonderful with ice cream or a whipped topping.

## About This Recipe

This is an easy and unconventional recipe that tastes better than it may sound. For those who are not drawn to traditional pumpkin pie, you will enjoy this recipe for its seasoning and texture. It is a baker friendly dish that is Randy's kind of recipe--little work with great results. Though she has been serving it for the last 5 years, she must admit that this Wisconsin recipe was handed down 2 generations to a dear friend of hers and then to her. She has served this dish to the delight of many including those who were not pumpkin fans. Try serving it warmed with a French Vanilla or Vanilla Bean ice cream--a perfect compliment to the crispy cobbler-type topping. For those who might miss the crust, feel free to slip one in underneath (or two considering the size of recipe). Best wishes for great eating!
