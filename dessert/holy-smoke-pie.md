# Holy Smoke Pie

**Contributor:** Sandy Regan

**Getty Entity:** Museum

**Department:** Visitor Services

---

## Servings & Ingredients

Serves 15 1 stick margarine, softened 1 cup all-purpose flour 3/4 cup chopped nuts 8 ozs. cream cheese 1 cup confectioners' sugar 3 cups Cool Whip 1 3-oz. box instant chocolate pudding 1 3-oz. box instant vanilla pudding 3 1/4 cup milk pecans, toasted (optional) chocolate, shaved (optional)

---

## Instructions

Combine margarine, flour and chopped nuts. Press into buttered 9 x 13 x 2" glass pan and bake at 325Â° F for 25 minutes. Cool. Combine cream cheese and confectioners' sugar; add 1 cup of Cool Whip and spread topping carefully on top of cool crust. Mix instant chocolate and vanilla pudding with milk. Spread on top of cream cheese mixture. Top with remaining 2 cups of Cool Whip. Optional: sprinkle with toasted pecans or shaved chocolate. Refrigerate until ready to serve.

## About This Recipe

When Sandy used to teach school she recieved this as a gift made by one of the parents for teaching her daughter over the summer. It has become a family favorite; at all family get-togethers her children, especially "the boys", request Holy Smoke Pie. Her children all live in other states, and when she goes to visit she always takes the recipe for this dessert.
