# Black as Midnight Chocolate Layer Cake

**Contributor:** Kari E. Johnson

**Getty Entity:** Conservation Institute

**Department:** Information and Communications

---

## Servings & Ingredients

Serves 8-10 Cake: 1 1/2 cups white sugar 1/2 cup light brown sugar 3/4 cup cocoa 2 teaspoons baking soda 1 teaspoon Rumford baking powder 1 teaspoon salt 2 extra large eggs 1 cup strong black coffee 1 cup buttermilk 1/2 cup vegetable oil or soft shortening 1 1/4 teaspoons vanilla extract Frosting: 3/4 cup unsalted butter, softened 5 1/3 cups confectioners' sugar, sifted 3/4 cup cocoa, sifted 1/2-3/4 cup half-and-half 2 teaspoons vanilla extract

---

## Instructions

Heat oven to 350Â° F. Line two 9-inch round cake pans with baking parchment. Grease and cocoa (instead of flour) the pans, including the baking parchment. In a large mixing bowl, sift together the sugars, flour, cocoa, baking soda, baking powder and salt. Add eggs, coffee, buttermilk, oil and vanilla. Beat 2 1/2 minutes on medium speed with an electric mixer. Pour batter into the prepared pans. Bake 25-35 minutes or until a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean. Cool 10 minutes. Remove the cakes from the pans, remove the baking parchment from their bottoms, and place cakes on wire racks. When completely cooled, frost with Dark Chocolate Buttercream Frosting. To make frosting, beat the softened butter with a wooden spoon or an electric mixer in a large mixing bowl until butter is light yellow. Add sifted confectioners' sugar and sifted cocoa alternately with the half-and-half. Beat until the ingredients are completely blended and the frosting is of spreading consistency.

Kari is an Abstractor/Indexer for Art and Archaeology Technical Abstracts in the Conservation Institute. She enjoys cooking but particularly loves to bake traditional "county fair" Americana desserts such as cakes, pies, coffee cakes, and quick breads. Kari takes a number of her baked goods to the Los Angeles County Fair for judging every summer and nearly always wins a prize for each entry.
