# Chocolate Covered Popcorn

**Contributor:** Jackie Zak

**Getty Entity:** GCI

**Department:** Information and Communications

---

## Servings & Ingredients

Serves 3-4 chocolate lovers per large mixing bowl 3 cups sugar 2/3 cups Hershey's Cocoa 1/8 teaspoon salt 1 1/2 cups milk 1/4 cup butter (1/2 stick) 1 teaspoon vanilla extract large mixing bowl popcorn

---

## Instructions

Stir sugar, cocoa, and salt together. Stir in milk. Cook over medium heat, stirring constantly until it comes to a full rolling boil. Boil without stirring until 234Â° F or forms a soft ball in cold water. Remove from heat and add butter and vanilla. Cool without stirring to room temperature (110Â° F). Beat with wooden spoon until it begins to thicken but still liquid enough to pour over popcorn. Mix chocolate into popcorn.

## About This Recipe

This is basically the recipe for fudge that used to be printed on the side of the Hershey's Cocoa can. The only variation is that the chocolate is not allowed to thicken. Most people find the thought of this snack disgusting until they taste it. As kids, Jackie and her sister considered it a delicacy to be served upon request for birthdays. They never grew up and still make it on rare occasions.
