## Instructions

Peel oranges (colored part only). Cut peel into 1/4-inch strips. Combine peel and enough water to cover in small saucepan and boil 3 minutes. Drain and repeat blanching process. Cook 2 cups water and sugar in heavy small saucepan over low heat until sugar dissolves, swirling pan occasionally. Bring to boil. Add blanched peel and simmer 20 minutes. Cool peel completely in syrup. Remove using slotted spoon. Cut candied peel into 1/4-inch dice. Set aside. Melt 2 tablespoons butter in heavy small skillet. Add pine nuts and stir until lightly browned, 3 to 4 minutes. Preheat oven to 325Â° F. Generously grease and flour 8-cup tube or Bundt pan. Using electric mixer, cream remaining butter. Add confectioners' sugar and beat until fluffy. Blend in vanilla and orange flower water. Beat in eggs and yolks one at a time. Combine flour with cornmeal, baking powder and salt. Gradually beat dry ingredients into creamed mixture. Fold candied orange peel and pine nuts into batter. Turn into prepared pan. Bake until tester inserted in center comes out clean, about 75 minutes. Transfer to rack. Dust with confectioners' sugar. Cool completely and set on platter.
