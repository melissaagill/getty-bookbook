# Mo's Magic Bars

**Contributor:** Monique Tagliaferri

**Getty Entity:** Trust

**Department:** Financial Management

---

## Servings & Ingredients

1/2 cup butter, melted slightly 1 1/2 cups graham cracker crumbs 1 14-oz. can condensed milk 1 cup semi-sweet chocolate chips half a bag of coconut (or more)

---

## Instructions

In a medium-sized bowl mix butter and graham cracker crumbs. Press into a 9 x 13" pan. Pour can of condensed milk into pan and smooth over crumb mixture. Sprinkle coconut over top along with chocolate chips. Bake at 450Â° F for 15-20 minutes or until top becomes firm.

## About This Recipe

Mo has worked for the Getty for over four years now. She has an MA in archaeology, loves softball, football, cats (she has two) and sweets! She found this recipe many years ago and takes it upon herself to make these bars as often as possible. They are great for holidays, birthdays and parties.
