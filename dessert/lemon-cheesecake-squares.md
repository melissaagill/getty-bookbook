# Lemon Cheesecake Squares

**Contributor:** Kathy Dunlop

**Getty Entity:** Trust

**Department:** Facilities Administration

---

## Servings & Ingredients

Yields 48 squares 1 package lemon-flavor pudding cake mix 1/2 cup butter 3 extra large eggs 2 8-oz. packages cream cheese, cut in chunks, room temperature 1/2 cup granulated sugar 1/2 teaspoon vanilla 2 teaspoons lemon juice confectioners' sugar

---

## Instructions

Heat oven to 350Â° F. Grease and flour a 13x9 inch baking pan. Mix cake mix, butter and 1 egg in large bowl with electric mixer until crumbly. Press in bottom of prepared pan. Bake 8 minutes, remove from oven. Meanwhile in a medium-sized bowl with electric mixer or in food processor beat or process cream cheese until smooth and fluffy. Mix in remaining 2 eggs, granulated sugar, vanilla and lemon juice until blended and smooth. Pour onto hot crust. Bake 25 minutes longer until top edges are golden brown. Dust with confectioners' sugar. Cool completely in pan on rack. Cut into squares. Store in refrigerator.

## About This Recipe

After high school, Kathy and her best friend would bake cookies together before the holidays to give as gifts. They would each pick four or five recipes, get together the Saturday before Christmas and bake all day. Then, they would split the goods to assemble gift bags and tins for friends and family. These made for inexpensive, but much appreciated gifts during her poor college days. She added Lemon Cheesecake Squares to her list about 15 years ago and they have become a requested favorite of her Getty colleagues year after year. Although she and her girlfriend have not baked together for years, she still fondly remembers those labor of love Saturdays in her kitchen.
