# "Big Easy" Pecan Pie

**Contributor:** Cathy Pericone

**Getty Entity:** Trust

**Department:** Human Resources

---

## Servings & Ingredients

1 frozen deep dish piecrust 4 extra large eggs 3/4 cup sugar 1/2 teaspoon salt 1 1/2 cups light corn syrup 1 tablespoon butter, melted 1 teaspoon vanilla extract 1 1/2 cups pecan pieces

---

## Instructions

Preheat the oven to 350Â° F. Using a wire whisk, beat the eggs together with the sugar and salt in a bowl. Add corn syrup, butter and vanilla and whisk until all ingredients are incorporated (The mixture should be frothy). Spread the pecan pieces in a layer on the bottom of the piecrust, pour in the filling. Bake for about an hour until firm.
