# Chocolate Mexican Wedding Cookies

**Contributor:** Kenneth Correa

**Getty Entity:** Research Institute

**Department:** Technical Services

---

## Servings & Ingredients

Yields 16 cookies 1 cup butter, softened 1/3 cup confectioners' sugar 2 teaspoons vanilla extract 1 3/4 cups all-purpose flour 1 cup ground pecans 1/2 cup sweet chocolate, grated 3/4 teaspoon ground cinnamon 1/2 teaspoon salt For dusting: 1/2 cup confectioners' sugar 1/4 cup sweet chocolate, grated and cocoa

---

## Instructions

In a large bowl, cream butter and 1/3 cup confectioners' sugar until light and fluffy. Add vanilla extract. In a separate bowl, combine flour, ground pecans, 1/2 cup grated sweet chocolate, cinnamon and salt; mix well. Gradually add dry ingredients to the creamed mixture. Wrap dough in plastic wrap and chill 1 to 2 hours, or until firm. Preheat oven to 325Â° F. Shape dough into 1 inch balls. Place balls 1 inch apart on an ungreased baking sheet. Bake 15 to 18 minutes, or until cookies are firm to the touch. Cool 1 minute on baking sheet, then transfer to a wire rack. For the coating, sift 1/2 cup of the confectioners' sugar and 1/4 cup of the grated sweet chocolate and cocoa into a shallow bowl. While cookies are still warm, roll them in the coating.

## About This Recipe

Ken has been working at the Getty for over 16 years. The last 15 years have been spent in the Acquisitions Section of the Technical Services Division of the GRI Library. It has been an interesting process to watch the Getty grow from a small family of employees into the institution it is today. He is happy to have been part of this process and looks forward to a long association with the Getty in the future. This recipe is a chocolate lover's twist on a favorite traditional Mexican cookie that first Ken's grandmother and then his mother used to make for special occasions and during the holidays, especially Christmas. He remembers as a child how wonderful the aroma of this sweet little pecan cookies used to fill the kitchen and the fun and excitement that his brothers, sister and he would have rolling these warm, fresh from the oven cookies into a bowl of confectioners' sugar and eating them with a large cup of Mexican cocoa. Double the batch so you have some for later!
