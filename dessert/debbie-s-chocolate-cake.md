# Debbie's Chocolate Cake

**Contributor:** Barbara Whitney

**Getty Entity:** Museum

**Department:** Administration

---

## Servings & Ingredients

First layer: 1 cup all-purpose flour 2 teaspoons baking powder 2 tablespoons cocoa 1/2 teaspoon salt 3/4 cup sugar 1/2 cup milk 2 tablespoons butter, melted 1 teaspoon vanilla Second layer: 1/2 cup sugar 1/2 cup brown sugar 3 tablespoons cocoa 1 cup water

---

## Instructions

Adjust oven rack to lower middle level and preheat oven to 350Â° F. Mix first layer ingredients together and pour into a greased baking pan (round or 8 x 8".) In a saucepan, boil second layer ingredients together for 5 minutes and then pour over the batter in the baking pan. Bake for 30-40 minutes. Cool on a rack.
