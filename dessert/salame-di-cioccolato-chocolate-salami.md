# Salame di Cioccolato (Chocolate Salami)

**Contributor:** Edith Ben-Horin

**Getty Entity:** Museum

**Department:** Bookstore

---

## Servings & Ingredients

Serves variable 1/2 cup raisins 7 ozs. semisweet (plain) chocolate 3 tablespoons butter 3 ozs. blanched almonds, finely chopped 7 ozs. petit beurre-type (sweet plain) biscuits, crushed 3 tablespoons mixed candied orange peel or other citrus peel 2 egg yolks

---

## Instructions

Soak raisins in warm water to cover until needed. Break up chocolate and place in saucepan or heatproof bowl with butter. Place over another saucepan of gently simmering water until melted. Add sugar, almonds, biscuit crumbs, drained raisins, and candied peel. Mix well and remove from heat. Stir in egg yolks and let mixture cool completely. Form mixture into salami shape with your hands and place it on a sheet of waxed paper. Seal paper around it and refrigerate for several hours. At serving time, remove paper. Slice the "salami" and arrange slices on a plate.


---

## About This Recipe

This rich dessert from the Emilia-Romagna region of central Italy really does look like salami when sliced. Variations can be made without raisins (allowing full attention to be paid to the rich chocolate taste), or, try adding cocoa powder to the mixture. Edith grew up in the northern city of Milan where the dessert is well known; this was her favorite dessert as a child. The Salame di Cioccolato made it to many birthday parties and, although it will never make the list of authentic birthday cakes, it will not leave taste buds or stomachs unsatisfied.
