# Crispy Oatmeal Cookies

**Contributor:** Angela Goncalves

**Getty Entity:** Trust

**Department:** President's Office

---

## Servings & Ingredients

2/3 cup butter or margarine 3/4 cup all-purpose flour 3/4 cup oatmeal 1 teaspoon baking powder 3 tablespoons maple syrup 3/4 cup sugar 1 extra large egg 2 tablespoons milk 2 teaspoons ground cinnamon or ginger

---

## Instructions

Place melted butter in a bowl. Stir in sugar and oatmeal. Sift together flour and baking powder and add to butter mixture. Stir in cinnamon or ginger, milk and syrup; mix until all ingredients are combined. Using teaspoons, drop small dollops of batter about 3 inches apart onto lightly greased baking sheet. Bake at 350Â° F for about 8 minutes or until cookies are thin and crispy and lightly browned around edges. Remove cookies immediately to cool. If you like cookies slightly rounded, place each over a wooden spoon or rolling pin while still warm. Cookies may be partially dipped into your choice of melted chocolate and left to cool before serving.
