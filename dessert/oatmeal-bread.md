# Oatmeal Bread

**Contributor:** Randy Fiel

**Getty Entity:** Trust

**Department:** Fitness Center

---

## Servings & Ingredients

Yields 2 loaves 2 packages active dry yeast 1/2 cup warm water 1 1/2 cups boiling water 1 cup quick cooking rolled oats 1/2 cup molasses or honey 1/2 cup shortening 1 tablespoon salt 6 1/4 cups all-purpose flour 2 extra large eggs, slightly beaten 3 tablespoons wheat germ

---

## Instructions

Soften active dry yeast in 1/2 cup warm water and set aside until needed. In large bowl combine 1 1/2 cups boiling water, rolled oats, molasses, shortening, and salt. Cool to lukewarm. Stir in 2 cups of flour and wheat germ; add eggs and beat well. Stir in softened yeast; beat well. Add remaining flour about 2 cups at a time, mixing vigorously after each addition, to make a moderately stiff dough. Mix vigorously until smooth, about 10 minutes. Grease top lightly. cover tightly; place in refrigerator at least 2 hours or overnight. Turn out on well-floured surface; shape in 2 loaves. Place in greased 8 1/2 x 4 1/2 x 2 1/2 inch loaf pans. Cover. Let double in warm place (about 2 hours). Bake in preheated oven (375Â° F) about 40 minutes.

## About This Recipe

Randy is a big fan of different types of bread and this is one of his favorites. It has a nice sweet taste to it yet tastes very light. Usually served by Randy's family during the holidays, Oatmeal Bread is a good compliment to egg nog.
