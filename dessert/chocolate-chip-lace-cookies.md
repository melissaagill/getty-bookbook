# Chocolate Chip Lace Cookies

**Contributor:** Jackie Burns

**Getty Entity:** Museum

**Department:** Photographic Services

---

## Servings & Ingredients

Yields 2 dozen 1 cup oatmeal (quick or old-fashioned) 1/2 cup sugar 1/2 cup brown sugar 2 tablespoons + 1 teaspoon all-purpose flour 1/4 teaspoon baking powder 1/4 teaspoon salt 1/2 teaspoon baking soda 1/2 cup butter, melted 1 extra large egg, beaten 1 1/2 teaspoons vanilla 3/4 cup chocolate chips 1/2 cup nuts, chopped (walnuts, pecans, or other nut of choice)

---

## Instructions

Preheat oven to 325Â° F. Mix dry ingredients. Pour melted butter over them. Add beaten egg and vanilla. Stir in chocolate chips and nuts. Drop small amounts (1 tsp.) on greased cookie sheet. Bake about 8-10 minutes, until golden brown. Carefully remove from pan when cooled.
