# Chocolate Crackle Cookies

**Contributor:** Julia Armstrong-Totten

**Getty Entity:** Research Institute

**Department:** Provenance Index

---

## Servings & Ingredients

Yields ca. 30 cookies 1 cup semisweet chocolate chips 1 cup brown sugar, packed 1/3 cup salad oil 2 extra large eggs 1 teaspoon vanilla 1 cup all-purpose flour 1 teaspoon baking powder 1/4 teaspoon salt 1/2 cup confectioners' sugar (or more if needed) optional: 1/2 cup walnuts, chopped

---

## Instructions

Melt chocolate chips carefully in microwave. Add remaining ingredients in the following order, mixing well after each addition: brown sugar, oil, eggs one at a time, vanilla, and combined dry ingredients. Stir in nuts last. Cover dough and chill at least 4 hours, but best if left overnight. Drop teaspoons of dough into the confectioners' sugar and roll to coat, shaping them into balls. Place on a greased cookie sheet and bake at 350Â° F for 10-15 minutes, until the cookies are firm to the touch but still soft. Remove immediately from cookie sheet and cool completely. Store in an airtight container. (To keep cookies soft and chewy, place an apple slice in storage container.)

## About This Recipe

These delicious soft and chewy brownies masquerading as cookies are a favorite amongst the Provenance Index staff. Julia used to make them almost weekly until she had a baby; now her daughter helps to measure and stir, but fewer cookies arrive at the office!
