# Apple Pie

**Contributor:** Andrew Taylor

**Getty Entity:** Research Institute

**Department:** Collection Development

---

## Servings & Ingredients

Serves 8 10 ozs. all-purpose flour 5 ozs. butter 2 tablespoons sugar cold water to mix 6 large apples (about 2 lbs) 4 ozs. golden raisins pinch grated nutmeg or ground cloves grated rind 1 lemon juice of half lemon or a dash of water 1 tablespoon sugar 1/2 pint heavy cream 2 tablespoons rose flower water 2 tablespoons sugar

---

## Instructions

Place flour, butter and sugar in food processor and mix until ingredients resemble breadcrumbs. With the food processor running, add water until the dough comes together. Allow to rest for an hour in the refrigerator. Core, peel and dice the apples into fairly large chunks, about 1-2 inches. Place in a saucepan with raisins, spice, lemon rind and juice, and sugar. Cook over low heat, adding a little water if necessary, until the apples are softened. The chunks should remain whole and not disintegrate into mush. Allow to cool. Note that too much spice or sugar obscures the flavor of the apples; ideally, the filling should be barely sweetened. Roll out 2/3 of the dough and line a 9-inch pie pan. Add the filling. Roll out the remaining dough and cover the pie. Crimp and seal the edges, decorate with pastry leaves. Bake in 400Â° F oven for about 1/2 hour. Remove and sprinkle more sugar on the piecrust while it is still hot. Whip the cream with sugar and rose water until very stiff, serve a generous dollop with each slice. The combination of lemon, apple, and roses is very exotic and luxurious.

## About This Recipe

Andrew moved to Los Angeles from the UK in 1993 to attend graduate school at UCLA, joining the Getty staff in 1998. His recipe is a British variant of an American classic: it is less sweet and uses cloves instead of cinnamon to bring out the flavor of the apples. What really makes the dish special is the flavored cream, which has an almost medieval taste - quite exotic and luxurious.  A common alternative filling is a combination of blackberries, apples and pears.
