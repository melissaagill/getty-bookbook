# Bread Pudding with Caramel Sauce

**Contributor:** Janine Mays

**Getty Entity:** Museum

**Department:** Information and Media Systems

---

## Servings & Ingredients

Serves 6 Bread Pudding: 4 cups day-old bread, cubed 1/2 cup raisins, chopped (optional) 2 cups evaporated milk 2 extra large eggs, whipped 2/3 cups packed brown sugar 1 1/2 teaspoons ground cinnamon 3/4 teaspoon ground nutmeg 1/4 teaspoon salt Caramel Sauce: 28 caramel squares 2 tablespoons bourbon 2 tablespoons water

---

## Instructions

Bread Pudding: In a large mixing bowl, combine milk, whipped eggs, brown sugar, spices, and salt. Mix well. Add cubed bread and raisins. Mix well until bread has softened. Soak bread in milk and eggs mixture for 15 min. Bake in a greased 2-qt shallow pan, uncovered at 350Â° F for about 1 hour or until knife inserted in center comes out clean. Caramel Sauce: Melt caramels with bourbon and water in heavy saucepan over low heat, stirring frequently until smooth.

## About This Recipe

Bread pudding was one of Janine's favorite childhood desserts. As an adult she still uses this recipe although she alternates between caramel and lemon sauce depending on her cravings. Janine's mom, Earline Parker, prepares her bread pudding using old bread (mostly end pieces that no one would eat). As the bread bin grew full of old bread Jainine knew it was just a matter of time before she would once again indulge in this delectable treat. Enjoy with real whipped cream in addition to the topping and a strong cup of brew.
