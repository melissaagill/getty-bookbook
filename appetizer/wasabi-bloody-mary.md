# Wasabi Bloody Mary

**Contributor:** Matthew De Marte

**Getty Entity:** Bon Appetit

**Department:** nan

---

## Servings & Ingredients

Serves 4 ice 1 teaspoon V8 Vegetable Juice 2 tablespoons A1 Steak Sauce 2 fresh limes 375 ml. Absolut Peppar Vodka wasabi Tabasco Pepper Sauce celery salt freshly ground pepper olives

---

## Instructions

Fill a gallon pitcher halfway with ice. Add the V8 juice, steak sauce, the juice from the 2 limes, vodka and wasabi, to taste. If you are not familiar with wasabi, be warned that it is incredibly powerful. Stir around mixture and add Tabasco sauce (just a few drops to start), celery salt (a few shakes) and the fresh ground pepper. Strain in to a white wineglass and garnish with a few olives. Everyone likes his or her Bloody Marys in different degrees of strength and spiciness. Do not be afraid to experiment.
