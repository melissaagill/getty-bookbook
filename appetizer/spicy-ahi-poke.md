# Spicy Ahi Poke

**Contributor:** Calvin Chan

**Getty Entity:** Trust

**Department:** Information Technology Services, Information Systems

---

## Servings & Ingredients

Serves 4 1 tablespoon Asian sesame oil 1 teaspoon togarashi (Japanese 7-spice) 1 tablespoon Coleman's mustard 1 tablespoon wasabi paste 2 tablespoons soy sauce 1 fresh Hawaiian or Thai chili 1 teaspoon salt 2 tablespoons spicy tobiko (flying fish roe) 2 tablespoons daikon sprouts (kaiware or radish) 1 tablespoon onion, minced 1 tablespoon scallion tops, minced 6 ozs. ahi tuna, cut into 1/4 inch dice Garnish: 2 tablespoons spicy tobiko 1 tablespoon white sesame seeds 1/4 cup scallion tops, minced

---

## Instructions

In a medium bowl, mix all ingredients except ahi tuna into a paste. Add cubes of ahi, stir to coat, and let sit at room temperature for 15 minutes. Sprinkle tobiko, sesame seeds and scallion over the top to garnish. The secret is to get the best quality Sushi grade fish (ahi tuna) you can.
