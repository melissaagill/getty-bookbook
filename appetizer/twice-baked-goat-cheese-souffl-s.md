## Servings & Ingredients

Serves 8 1 cup fine fresh breadcrumbs 3 tablespoons unsalted butter 3 tablespoons all-purpose flour 3/4 cup milk 8 ozs. soft mild goat cheese (such as Montrachet), crumbled 3 large egg yolks 7 large egg whites
