## About This Recipe

Although Isabella's papa is a brilliant gourmet chef, when it comes time to hosting a tea party she finds her Aunt Muz's exotic concoctions just the thing with their novelty, simplicity, and pretty presentation. Construct sandwiches with their particular ingredients adjacent as listed for optimum pleasing taste combination. Use Pepperidge Farm Very Thin Sliced Wheat and Very Thin Sliced White breads with crusts removed, and a sourdough baguette. A special touch is to cut sandwich bread with different metal cookie cutters; suggested shapes follow each sandwich. Serve with a hearty Keemun tea such as Prince of Wales or, alternatively, a flowery tea such as Yin Hao Jasmine.
