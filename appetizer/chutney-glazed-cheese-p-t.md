## Servings & Ingredients

Serves 6-8 6 ozs. cream cheese at room temperature 4 ozs. cheddar cheese, shredded 3 tablespoons dry sherry 3/4 teaspoon curry powder dash salt 1/2 cup mango chutney, chopped 1/4 cup scallions, chopped

---

## Instructions

Beat together the cream and cheddar cheeses. (Libby recommends a Cuisinart for all shredding and mixing.) Blend in curry powder and salt to taste. Heat sherry and add to cheese mixture; blend until smooth. Spread mixture 1/2-inch thick on a serving plate; chill until firm. At serving time, spread mango chutney over the top and sprinkle with finely-chopped scallions.
