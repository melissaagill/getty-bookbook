## Instructions

Preheat the oven to 400Â° F. In a saucepan, combine milk and butter. Bring liquid to a slow boil. Add flour, salt, and pepper and stir quickly with a wooden spoon until smooth, over medium to low heat, about 1 minute. Remove from heat. Stir in eggs, one at a time, incorporating each egg before adding the next. Stir in cheese. On a well greased or parchment-lined baking sheet, place a spoonful of the mixture, about 1/4" apart. Bake for 10 minutes, then reduce heat to 350Â° F. Continue to bake for 25 minutes or until firm to the touch. Serve warm. These are very versatile. Bigger spoonfuls will make larger rolls that can be cut open and stuffed for sandwiches. Small rolls may be served as appetizers, or as accompaniments to the main dish. Experiment with different cheeses (a smoked Gouda is great), add fresh herbs (thyme or tarragon), or add bits of flaked salmon and dill.
