# Toad in the Hole

**Contributor:** Tim McNeil

**Getty Entity:** Museum

**Department:** Exhibitions Design

---

## Servings & Ingredients

Serves 4 5-6 large sausages (not breakfast links) 3 extra large eggs 1 1/2 cups milk 1 1/2 cups unbleached all-purpose flour 1 teaspoon salt

---

## Instructions

Set oven to 450Â° F. Place sausages in a lightly oiled 9" x 13" pan and cook for 5 mins. In a bowl beat eggs thoroughly; gradually add in milk, flour, and salt, beating until well blended. Pour batter into hot pan with sausages and bake 25-30 minutes until the batter rises and browns on top. Serve hot; good with gravy.


---

## About This Recipe

Based on Yorkshire pudding, this dish is traditionally served in Yorkshire, England as an appetizer with onion gravy. However, it can also be served as a main dish with vegetables. Growing up in Yorkshire, it was cooked for Tim by both his grandmother and mother. The true test of a good "toad" is the height the batter rises to when cooked, and the quality of the sausage used. Other variations on its name include "sausage toad" and "frog in the pit".
