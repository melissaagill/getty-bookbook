# Crab-Stuffed Mushrooms

**Contributor:** Karen Mitchell

**Getty Entity:** Trust

**Department:** Administration

---

## Servings & Ingredients

Makes 2 dozen 1 cup fresh crabmeat (may substitute with canned crabmeat) 2 cups cheddar cheese, grated 1 cup Parmesan cheese, grated 3 green onions (green portion only) approx. 1/2 cup mayonnaise 2 dozen medium-sized button mushrooms

---

## Instructions

Pull crabmeat apart into small pieces. Combine crabmeat and cheddar cheese in a mixing bowl. Chop the green onions, using only the green portions of the onion, and add to crab mixture. Add Parmesan cheese. Slowly begin to add mayonnaise to the mixture--you want the ingredients to just hold together so that the consistency of the mixture is like that of cookie dough. Remove stems from mushrooms and discard. Clean mushrooms in cold water. Line a cookie sheet with foil, and place a baking rack over it so the mushrooms will be elevated from the cookie sheet. Fill the mushroom caps with crab mixture. Be careful not to overfill. Place cookie sheet with baking rack in broiler and cook for approximately 7-10 minutes or until filling begins to bubble and brown, and mushrooms appear cooked. (For best results do not microwave.) Let cool for approximately 5 minutes and serve. If you are not a mushroom lover, this mixture is delicious on rye or sourdough bread, or in a premade filo dough shell. (The time for broiling may be less on these other items, so keep an eye on them.)

## About This Recipe

This recipe was a favorite of Karen's college friends. Whenever there was a party, they always begged her to make these mushrooms. Additionally, they would ask her to double the size of the ingredients, because the next day they would make baked potatoes stuffed with the mixture.  Chardonnay from the Napa Valley compliments this dish nicely.
