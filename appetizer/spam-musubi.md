# Spam Musubi

**Contributor:** Lynne Kaneshiro

**Getty Entity:** Museum

**Department:** Paper Conservation

---

## Servings & Ingredients

Serves 8 3 cups cooked rice (not minute rice) 1 can Spam, cut into 8 slices 1/4 cup soy sauce 1/4 cup sugar 4 sheets nori (pressed dried seaweed)

---

## Instructions

Fry Spam slices until browned, add soy sauce and sugar mixture. Cook for a few minutes. Cut nori sheets in half. Press rice into oblong, Spam can shapes. Place a layer of shaped rice in the middle of a 1/2 sheet of nori, then top with a Spam slice, then add more rice. Fold nori over.

## About This Recipe

What do you expect from someone who speaks Pidgin English when home with family and friends? Hawaii is not only hula dancers, leis and pineapples, but the Spam capital of the world. Whenever Lynne is home for a visit, inevitably there is a family get-together with such ono foods as laulau, haupia, pupu chicken, lomi-lomi salmon, malasadas and mochi. However she does not lick her lips in anticipation until she spies the usual huge platter of Spam musubi. Lynne does not care to eat Spam on the mainland, but in Hawaii it does not feel like being home until she has at least one plateful of these rice balls.
