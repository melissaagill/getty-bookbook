# The Original Shrimp Cebiche

**Contributor:** Helen Mauchi

**Getty Entity:** Conservation Institute

**Department:** Information and Communications

---

## Servings & Ingredients

Serves 6-8 2 lbs. medium-sized shrimp (uncooked with shell) 1/2 cup lime juice, freshly squeezed 1/4 cup orange juice, freshly squeezed 1 teaspoon mustard 1/2 white onion, thinly sliced 1/2 bunch parsley (or to taste), chopped very thinly 1 teaspoon white sugar 8 medium-sized organic tomatoes salt, pepper, and Tabasco hot sauce to taste

---

## Instructions

Prepare 12 hours before serving. Preparation time: 2 hours. A key ingredient is a tomato sauce freshly made from scratch. To do so, place tomatoes in rolling hot water for a few minutes until slightly soft and semi-firm (do not over boil to a mushy texture). Peel tomatoes and mash in food processor, blender or by hand; sift sauce. Slice onions very thinly and "cook" or "cure" them by rubbing raw onion slices with approximately 1/2 teaspoon of salt. Allow salted onions to sit for about 15 minutes at room temperature until they turn a bit transparent and soft in texture. In the meantime, carefully slice the top part of the unshelled raw shrimp and devein. Place unshelled, deveined, raw shrimp in rolling boiling water. Remove shrimp from boiling water once it turns orange/white (do not overcook meat). Reserve a cup of shrimp flavored water. Sift shrimp and peel shells. In a large bowl mix one cup of shrimp flavored water, tomato sauce, lime and orange juices, salt and pepper to taste, a few dashes of Tabasco hot sauce, chopped parsley, cured chopped onions, mustard, and sugar. Once all the ingredients above are mixed, add the shrimp to the concoction. Refrigerate Shrimp Cebiche for twelve hours before serving. Cebiche is traditionally served with toasted corn and bread on the side.

## About This Recipe

Helen Mauchi is a senior designer at GCI's Information & Communications unit. Although born and raised in Ecuador, Helen is of Chinese descent. Helen's family has been involved in the restaurant business for several generations, a background that has given her a vigorous appreciation of good food as well as a few culinary secrets. Cebiche is a world famous seafood-tomato-sauce-based cold appetizer. It is originally from Peru, a country blessed with one of the richest marine resources in the world and a wonderful repertoire of seafood dishes. Cebiche can be served anytime, especially during hot summer days. Although there are many styles of cebiche around the world, this particular recipe is one of the finest and most authentic. Indeed, it is a secret family recipe, handed down for generations from a distinguished Peruvian criollo family and now revealed to the world for a noble cause.
