# Spicy Shrimp with Holy Basil

**Contributor:** Murtha Baca

**Getty Entity:** Research Institute

**Department:** Standards & Research Projects

---

## Servings & Ingredients

Serves 4 1 teaspoon white peppercorns 2 garlic cloves, finely chopped 1 1/2 tablespoons cilantro, coarsely chopped 1 tablespoon fresh ginger, minced 2 to 6 fresh Thai chilis, minced 1 1/2 teaspoons finely grated lime zest (peel) 2 tablespoons vegetable or olive oil 1 lb. medium-sized shrimp, shelled and deveined 2 tablespoons soy sauce "holy basil" (Thai basil, also called gaprao) (if you can't find this, use half Italian basil and half mint leaves, torn)

---

## Instructions

In a small dry skillet, toast the peppercorns over moderate heat just until fragrant, about 1 minute. Transfer to a mortar and coarsely crush. Add the garlic, cilantro, ginger, chiles, lime zest, and salt, and pound to a coarse paste. Heat the oil in a large skillet or wok. Add the paste and stir-fry over high heat for 30 seconds. Add the shrimp and stir-fry until opaque throughout, 2-5 minutes. Stir in soy sauce. Transfer to a bowl, sprinkle with torn fresh basil leaves, and serve. Goes well with jasmine rice or soba noodles.
