# Spring, Summer, Anytime Sangria

**Contributor:** Andrea Leonard

**Getty Entity:** Museum

**Department:** Visitor Services

---

## Servings & Ingredients

Serves 4 2 large washed juice oranges (1 orange sliced; 1 juiced) 1 large washed lemon, sliced 1/4 cup sugar 1/4 cup Triple Sec 1 750 milliliter bottle of inexpensive (trust me), fruity, medium-bodied red wine, chilled

---

## Instructions

Add sliced orange, lemon, and sugar to large pitcher; mash gently with wooden spoon until fruit releases some juice but is not totally crushed, and sugar dissolves, about 1 minute. Stir in orange juice, Triple Sec, and wine; refrigerate for at least 2 but not more than 8 hours (5 is ideal). Stir before serving. Andrea adds slightly less Triple Sec than 1/4 cup and then does not add any ice when serving. This recipe is adapted from "Cook's Illustrated" May/June 1998.


---

## About This Recipe

Andrea first served this at a friend's party (she made a double batch) and it was gone in 3 minutes (literally). Since this friend loves to have parties she had to buy several pitchers and discovered that a quadruple batch is almost enough for a party of 10, it is that good.
