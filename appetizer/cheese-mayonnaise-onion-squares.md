# Cheese-Mayonnaise-Onion Squares

**Contributor:** Carolyn Higgens

**Getty Entity:** Conservation Institute

**Department:** Director's Office

---

## Servings & Ingredients

Serves 6 6 slices of white bread (Pepperidge Farms works best), each slice cut into 4 squares with crusts removed 1/2 cup Swiss cheese (Emmenthal or GruyÃ¨re), grated 1/2 cup mayonnaise (preferably Best Foods - not homemade) 1/2 cup white onions, minced

---

## Instructions

Preheat oven to 400Â° F. Mix together grated cheese, onions and mayonnaise in a small bowl. Spread a spoonful of mixture on each square of bread, covering evenly. Bake on an ungreased sheet for 10 minutes or until golden brown. Serve. Go back to the kitchen and make a second batch.

## About This Recipe

These cheese squares were always the most popular hors d'oeuvre served at the hundreds of cocktail parties hosted by Carolyn's parents while her father was in the diplomatic service abroad. Carolyn's mother has been making them for over 30 years. They were a multi-cultural hit; no matter where they lived (Africa, Asia, Europe, New Zealand, Australia or the USA), they were gobbled up as soon as they came from the oven. They are easy to make, do not require any unusual ingredients or equipment, take only a few minutes to whip up and go very well with drinks.
