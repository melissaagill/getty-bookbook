# Gallery Teacher Appetizer

**Contributor:** Anne Spackman

**Getty Entity:** Museum

**Department:** Education

---

## Servings & Ingredients

Serves 12 or so 1 14-oz. can artichoke hearts, not marinated 1 1/2 cups mozarella cheese, shredded 1 cup Parmesan cheese, grated 1 cup mayonnaise dash of garlic powder paprika crackers

---

## Instructions

Chop artichoke hearts in a large bowl. Add cheeses, mayonnaise, and garlic powder and stir. Transfer to greased casserole dish and sprinkle with paprika. Bake for about 15 minutes at 350Â° F. Serve with crackers.

## About This Recipe

This appetizer has been Anne's contribution to dozens of Education Department potlucks over the past eighteen years. There have yet to be any leftovers!
