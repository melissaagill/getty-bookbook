## Instructions

Adjust oven rack to lowest level and pre-heat oven to 400Â° F. Put everything except the Sambucca into a roasting pan and toss. Roast uncovered for an hour, stirring every 20 minutes. Before the last 20 minutes stir in the Sambucca. The peppers should burn a bit at the edges. Serve as a side dish with roast lamb, turkey, chicken or beef. Also good cold.
