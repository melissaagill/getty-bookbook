# Bacon and Endive Salad

**Contributor:** Dena Carter

**Getty Entity:** Museum

**Department:** Bookstore

---

## Servings & Ingredients

Serves 2 6 slices of bacon, fried crisp (save the grease) 1 bunch washed endive, torn into small pieces (field greens may be substituted) 1/2 sweet onion, chopped 1 tiny jar pimentos 1/2 cup sugar red vinegar (just enough to dissolve the sugar) salt to taste

---

## Instructions

Put cleaned torn endive into salad bowl. Fry the bacon until crisp. Crumble the bacon and set aside. Add sugar to the bacon grease and enough vinegar to dissolve it. Heat for about 1 minute. SautÃ© onions in this mixture until they are soft and translucent. If it looks too dry, add virgin olive oil. Pour the mixture over the endive; add the bacon and pimentos. Salt to taste. Serve warm with a thick crusted bread.


---

## About This Recipe

This recipe was given to Dena by her father, Gary Carter. In the late 40s and early 50s he remembers accompanying his mother to harvest dandelions in the fields. They would dig them up in spring before they flowered, wash them and dress them simply with either oil and vinegar or the dressing that follows. Now he uses the same dressing for endive since no one digs dandelion greens anymore.
