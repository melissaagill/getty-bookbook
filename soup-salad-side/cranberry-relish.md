# Cranberry Relish

**Contributor:** Jean Green

**Getty Entity:** Museum

**Department:** Bookstore

---

## Servings & Ingredients

Serves 6-8 1 bag fresh cranberries 1 cup sugar 1 cup water 1/2 cup walnuts 1/2 cup golden raisins 1/4 teaspoon ground cinnamon 1/8 teaspoon ground cloves

---

## Instructions

Heat water and sugar in a large saucepan until sugar is dissolved. Add cranberries and cook for ten minutes, stirring gently. Remove from heat and cool. In small bowl mix cranberry sauce, half the chopped walnuts, raisins, cinnamon and cloves. Cover and chill cranberry mixture thoroughly in refrigerator. Stir again before serving. Sprinkle the remaining chopped walnuts over the cranberry mixture before serving. Alternatively, pecans may be used.


---

## About This Recipe

The most requested recipe by Jean's family and friends during the holiday season, it is a wonderful condiment for turkey or pork roast. She always presents it in a festive way: a garnish of edible flowers or extra nuts sprinkled on top. It looks beautiful, tastes great and the wonderful aroma of cloves and cinnamon permeate the house.
