# Jean's Crab Salad

**Contributor:** Jackie Goldfield

**Getty Entity:** Museum

**Department:** Education

---

## Servings & Ingredients

Serves 4-8 1 1/2 lbs. real or imitation crab flakes 1 celery rib, chopped 1/4 onion, chopped 2 tablespoon fresh parsley, chopped 2 tablespoon light mayonnaise 1 teaspoon lemon juice 1 tablespoon ranch dressing

---

## Instructions

Mix all ingredients and refrigerate until ready to serve.


---

## About This Recipe

Jackie's mother, Jean, came up with this recipe by putting together a little of this and a little of that, so all amounts are approximate.
