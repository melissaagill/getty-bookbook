# Orzo with Kalamatas, etc.

**Contributor:** Karen Giles

**Getty Entity:** Museum

**Department:** Education

---

## Servings & Ingredients

Serves 4 1 cup orzo 16 pitted kalamata olives, finely chopped 2 cloves garlic, minced 1 tablespoon sun-dried tomato paste 1/4 cup parsley, chopped 2 tablespoons olive oil 1/4 cup pine nuts, toasted freshly ground black pepper

---

## Instructions

Cook the orzo according to package directions and drain thoroughly. Mix olives, garlic, tomato paste, and parsley in a large bowl. Toss hot orzo with olive oil; add olive mixture, then pine nuts and pepper. Serve and enjoy!
