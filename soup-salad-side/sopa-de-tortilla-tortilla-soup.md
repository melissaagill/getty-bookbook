# Sopa de Tortilla (Tortilla Soup)

**Contributor:** Timothy Cameron

**Getty Entity:** Research Institute

**Department:** Library Technical Services

---

## Servings & Ingredients

Yields 3 quarts 8 cups chicken broth 2 boneless, skinless, cooked chicken breasts, shredded 1 red onion, diced 2 carrots, peeled and diced 1 potato, peeled and diced 1/4 cup tomato paste 1 bay leaf 2 cloves of garlic, chopped 1/4 teaspoon canned chipotle chili (or more) 1 teaspoon seeded minced jalapeÃ±o 1 teaspoon dried oregano 1/8 teaspoon cumin 1 teaspoon salt 1 teaspoon white pepper 1 small zucchini, diced 1 small yellow squash, diced 1 small red bell pepper, diced 1 tablespoon fresh cilantro, chopped 1 tablespoon fresh mint, chopped 2 tablespoons fresh squeezed lime juice 2 tablespoons sweet sherry For garnish: 4 (or more) corn tortillas, cut into thin strips (or use store-bought corn tortilla chips) 1 to 2 cups vegetable oil, for frying your own chips 8 oz. grated Monterey jack cheese 2 avocados, sliced 1/2 cup fresh cilantro, chopped

---

## Instructions

Combine chicken broth, shredded chicken, onion, carrots, potato, tomato paste, bay leaf, garlic, chipotle chili, jalapeÃ±o, oregano and cumin in a large stockpot on high heat. Bring to a boil, reduce heat and let simmer for 20 minutes. Add salt and white pepper, squash and bell pepper. Bring to a boil again, reduce and simmer for 15 minutes. Add cilantro, mint, lime juice and sherry. For the garnish, fry your own tortilla strips in vegetable oil, if desired. Serve the soup and you and your guests may garnish with tortilla chips, cheese, avocado and cilantro to taste.

## About This Recipe

Tim works as a Senior Acquisitions Assistant in Technical Services. Tim's partner Jimmy received this recipe from his aunt, who says she got it from an El Torito restaurant (though this has not been confirmed). It has been one of their favorite soups since they first tried it, and has always been a big hit when served to their family and friends. This tasty soup works well as a main course for lunch or as a first course or side dish for dinner. It is not hard to make, but the long list of ingredients does require some extra preparation time.
