# Meat Sauce for Pasta

**Contributor:** Barry Munitz

**Getty Entity:** Trust

**Department:** President

---

## Servings & Ingredients

2 lbs. ground meat (sirloin preferred) 1 cup modest red wine 1/4 cup red wine vinegar 1/2 cup Heinz ketchup 1 tablespoon raw mustard fine powder 1/2 onion, chopped very fine 1 sliced clove of garlic 1/2 cup cooking oil 1/4 cup oregano

---

## Instructions

The Magic Process: take wide frying pan; simmer oil in pan over low heat while rubbing garlic into oil. When pan is hot put in onions, then meat. Still on low heat, pour in 1/2 cup wine, 1/4 cup vinegar and stir. After 10 minutes, mix mustard into 1/2 cup ketchup and pour over meat; stir over low flame. After 10 more minutes, add remaining wine and cover pan. Still low heat, simmer 15 minutes while stirring, sprinkle oregano. Cover with intermittent stirring until meat is cooked through.
