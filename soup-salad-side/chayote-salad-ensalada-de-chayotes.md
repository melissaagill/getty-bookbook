# Chayote Salad (Ensalada de Chayotes)

**Contributor:** Amy Wilkins

**Getty Entity:** Research Institute

**Department:** Administration

---

## Servings & Ingredients

Serves 6 2 lbs. chayotes salt 6 tablespoons olive oil 3 tablespoons balsamic vinegar 1/4 teaspoon ground pepper 1/2 teaspoon salt 1 teaspoon dried oregano 1/2 red onion, thinly sliced and separated into rings

---

## Instructions

Place chayotes unpeeled in a large saucepan. Cover with water, add a pinch of salt and cook covered 30-40 minutes until soft. Drain and let cool. Peel chayotes, cut in half, then cut each half into 3-4 strips. Chill. Combine the oil, vinegar, salt, pepper and oregano. Stir well. Mix in chayote, top with onions. Let stand for 5 minutes before serving.

Amy loves Mexican food and enjoys experimenting with many different flavors and ingredients. A friend told her about chayote in a wonderful soup recipe. After tasting it, Amy thought about using it in a salad. The results are delicious.
