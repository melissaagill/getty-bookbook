# Spinach and Artichoke Casserole

**Contributor:** Ellen Rundle

**Getty Entity:** Trust

**Department:** Information Technology Services, Client Services

---

## Servings & Ingredients

Serves 6 2 packages frozen artichoke hearts (or 2 cans, but not marinated type) 1 package frozen spinach 1 8-oz. package cream cheese, softened 2 tablespoons mayonnaise salt and pepper Parmesan cheese, grated

---

## Instructions

Steam the vegetables separately. Cut the artichoke hearts into smaller chunks and place on the bottom of a medium-sized baking dish. Salt and pepper. Then cover with steamed spinach. In a small mixing bowl blend together cream cheese and mayonnaise. If you are watching fat content, buy the light varieties of these ingredients. Pour and spread over the spinach. Generously cover with Parmesan cheese. Bake at 350Â° F for 40 minutes or until brown.
