# Wait a minute, this isn't just Jell-O...

**Contributor:** Debra Canter

**Getty Entity:** Research Institute

**Department:** Web Production

---

## Servings & Ingredients

Serves 8 1/2 seedless navel orange 1 3-oz. box cherry Jell-O (regular or sugar free) 1 1/2 cups boiling water 1 cup cold water 1 8-oz. can whole berry cranberry sauce 1 tablespoon fresh lemon juice 1/4 teaspoon ground cinnamon 1/8 teaspoon ground cloves 1/4 cup walnuts, chopped

---

## Instructions

Peel the orange and eat half of it. Take the other half and remove the white pithy skin from each segment (trust me on this one, it's not that difficult.) Cut each section into 3 or 4 pieces and set aside. Mix Jell-O with 1 1/2 cups boiling water, until dissolved. Add 1 cup cold water. Pour Jell-O into mold or any shallow serving dish that is approximately 8 x 8" and refrigerate 15-30 minutes. In a separate bowl mix the cranberry sauce, lemon juice, cinnamon, cloves, orange and walnuts. As soon as Jell-O begins to set (look for a very light jiggle), add the rest of the ingredients into Jell-O mold and give it a light stir to distribute the yummy surprises. Stick the whole thing back in the fridge until it's completely set. You can double this recipe easily and best of all, you can make it the night before.

## About This Recipe

Debra is addicted to the Iron Chef. Although she has a huge collection of cookbooks, they are used solely for inspiration. When she reads "top pancakes with bananas and serve", she interprets "broil the bananas, add cinnamon and cream cheese, and stuff it into french toast," but oops, she's only supposed to include one recipe. Back to the dish... serve it with everything, goes especially well with turkey. A dollop of this "Jell-O" in a turkey sandwich the next day is heaven! (Oops another recipe.) To call this dish "Jell-O" is misleading; when Debra serves this and inquires, "Have you tried the Jell-O?", she always gets a polite "not yet", then she gives them a forkful, and always gets an enthusiastic "Hey, what's in this, can I have the recipe, this is delicious!"
