# Ampalaya (Bitter Melon) Salad

**Contributor:** Adonis "DJ" DeJesus

**Getty Entity:** Trust

**Department:** Facilities, Logistics

---

## Servings & Ingredients

400 g ampalaya (bitter melon) 1 tablespoon sea salt 150 g cooked shrimp, shelled and de-veined (optional) 1 large onion, sliced 1 tablespoon vinegar 3 tablespoons olive oil 1/2 teaspoon granulated sugar more sea salt to taste tomato wedges for garnish

---

## Instructions

Cut the bitter melon in half lengthwise; remove and discard the white pith and seeds, then cut diagonally. Mash with salt and squeeze the bitter juice, then wash well in running water, squeeze dry and place in a salad bowl. Add shrimp and onion. Prepare vinaigrette with the vinegar, olive oil, sugar, salt and pepper and add the to salad bowl. Combine well. Refrigerate (serve cold). Garnish with tomato wedges just before serving. To add pizzaz, add salted duck eggs cut into thin wedges.

D.J. De Jesus is a Supervisor in Logistics and an original start-up member of the Facilities Management Team.
