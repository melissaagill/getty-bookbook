# The Quick Chicken Noodle Alternative

**Contributor:** Kimberli Waack

**Getty Entity:** Research Institute

**Department:** Administration

---

## Servings & Ingredients

2 chicken breasts, sliced 6-8 cups of water 1 can or small frozen package of corn 1 large can tomato sauce 1 can black beans, drained 1 small to medium-sized onion, diced 1 1/2 cups whole grape tomatoes or Roma tomatoes, sliced juice of 1 lemon 1 tablespoon chili powder dried red peppers (optional) 1 package corn tortillas cheddar cheese, grated

---

## Instructions

Bake chicken in a covered dish. While chicken bakes combine water, corn, black beans, onion, tomatoes, and tomato sauce in a large pot. Add chili powder and lemon juice. Add peppers at this point if you are using them. Bring to a boil and let simmer. Once onions and tomatoes are almost fully cooked, add sliced baked chicken breasts. As the soup is simmering, slice the corn tortillas into bite-sized strips. Spread in a thin layer on a cookie sheet and place into an oven at 350Â° F. Bake until crispy. Serve soup topped with cheese and tortilla strips. Salt and pepper if you desire.
