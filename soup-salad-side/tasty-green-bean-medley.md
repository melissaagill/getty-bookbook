# Tasty Green Bean Medley

**Contributor:** Jim Bullock

**Getty Entity:** Trust

**Department:** Facilities, Director's Office

---

## Servings & Ingredients

Serves 6 1 15-oz. can French style sliced green beans 1 15-oz. can whole kernel corn, drained 1/2 cup mayonnaise 1/2 cup sharp cheddar cheese, shredded 1/2 cup celery, chopped 1/2 cup green pepper, chopped 2 tablespoons onion, minced salt and pepper buttered breadcrumbs (croutons)

---

## Instructions

Preheat oven to 350Â° F. In a large bowl combine all ingredients and place in 9 x 9 x 2" baking dish. Sprinkle buttered croutons over top of casserole. Bake uncovered for 30 minutes, or until croutons are golden.

## About This Recipe

This is one of the first dishes passed to the newlywed Bullocks in early 1976 by Jim Bullock's mom, Isabelle. This recipe has been a mainstay for many years at Thanksgiving and holiday family meals. Jim Bullock's wife, Toot, is especially fond of this recipe. It is very easy to prepare, cooks quickly, and as its name suggests, is very tasty. It is also a very good dish to take to a potluck dinner party.
