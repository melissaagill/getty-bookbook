# Grilled Chicken and Feta Salad

**Contributor:** Andrew Werner

**Getty Entity:** Research Institute

**Department:** Administration

---

## Servings & Ingredients

Serves 4 4 fat (i.e. not puny filets) boneless chicken breasts 1 solid block of feta cheese 2 red bell peppers 2 yellow bell peppers mixed greens balsamic vinegar salt fresh ground pepper

---

## Instructions

Slice the center of chicken breasts lengthwise 3/4 of the way through, stuff with slices of feta and seal open edge with toothpick. Season chicken with salt and pepper. Grill stuffed breasts until lightly browned and fully cooked. Slice and remove seeds from bell peppers. Grill strips of pepper, skin side to flame until skin is blackened. Allow peppers to cool slightly, remove and discard skin. Arrange greens on four salad plates and sprinkle with balsamic vinegar. Place bell pepper slices over greens alternating red and yellow strips. Place grilled chicken breast on top and remove toothpick. Serve while chicken is still hot.
