# Southern Succulence

**Contributor:** Donna Beckage

**Getty Entity:** Research Institute

**Department:** Integrated Research

---

## Servings & Ingredients

Serves 6-8 6 cups yellow crookneck squash, sliced 1/2 cup onion, chopped 1/2 teaspoon salt 1/2 cup margarine, melted 1 8-oz. package stuffing mix 1 10-oz. can cream of chicken soup 1/2 cup sour cream 1 cup carrots, grated 4-oz. jar artichoke hearts, sliced

---

## Instructions

Preheat oven to 350Â° F. Grease 2-quart casserole. Cook squash and onions in a small amount of salted water for about 5 minutes. Combine margarine and stuffing mix. Drain squash and onion, add soup and sour cream, then fold in carrots and artichokes. In prepared casserole dish place alternate layers of stuffing mix and squash mixture, beginning and ending with stuffing mix. Bake 25 minutes.


---

## About This Recipe

While enjoying dinner at her cousin's home in rural Tennessee some years ago, Donna first tasted this delicious casserole served with catfish and hush puppies. Soon it became one of her favorite dishes to contribute at holiday feasts and potluck meals. Southern Succulence has been served at more than one birthday lunch at the Research Institute.
