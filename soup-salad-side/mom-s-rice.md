# Mom's Rice

**Contributor:** Robin Lilien

**Getty Entity:** Museum

**Department:** Information and Media Systems

---

## Servings & Ingredients

Serves 4 1/2 stick unsalted margarine several handfuls of fine egg noodles (Manischewitz) 1 cup rice 5 chicken bouillon cubes (Herb-ox preferred) 2 1/2 cups boiling water

---

## Instructions

Melt the margarine over medium heat in a large pan. Start the water boiling while the margarine is melting. When the margarine is melted, add the egg noodles and brown them. Stir almost constantly (a wooden spoon works well) so that they do not burn. Lower heat as needed to avoid burning. When the noodles are golden brown, pour in the rice and bouillon cubes followed by the boiling water. Lower the heat immediately to low and cover the pan. Simmer until all of the water is absorbed. Check and stir the rice often to avoid sticking to the pan. This recipe can be doubled, tripled as needed.


---

## About This Recipe

This recipe from Robin's childhood was made by her mother on special occasions. It goes well with meat: chicken, beef, or lamb. (Sorry to all the vegetarians out there) Robin generally makes this on Friday nights for Shabbat dinner with her family, and they love it. She also enjoys serving it to guests. It is relatively easy to make, but guests will consider it elaborate. If you transfer the rice to a covered casserole dish for serving, it will stay warm throughout the meal.
