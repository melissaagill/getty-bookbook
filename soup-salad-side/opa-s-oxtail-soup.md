# Opa's Oxtail Soup

**Contributor:** Anne-Mieke Halbrook

**Getty Entity:** Research Institute

**Department:** Collection Management

---

## Servings & Ingredients

Serves 4 For broth: 1 oxtail of 2-3 lbs 1 large carrot 1 stalk celery 1 small onion 2 bay leaves water peppercorns and salt to taste For filling: 4 small potatoes 2 carrots 2 stalks celery 1/2 head of green cabbage 2 ears fresh corn, husked and halved tortillas queso ranchero lemon wedges salsa cilantro, chopped

---

## Instructions

Rinse oxtails and put in a large pot with water to cover. Add sliced carrot, celery, onion, bay leaves, salt and peppercorns. Bring to boil and simmer for 2 1/2 to 3 hours or until meat is done. (If you have a pressure cooker, use that for cooking the meat since it reduces the cooking time to 1 - 1 1/2 hrs). When the meat is tender, set it aside and strain the broth. Discard vegetables. Return meat to strained broth and add potatoes cut in quarters, corn halves, sliced carrots and celery and shredded cabbage. Bring to boil and simmer for about 15 minutes, or until the potatoes are cooked. Serve meat and vegetables in shallow bowls with warm tortillas, salsa, lemon wedges, chopped cilantro and ranchero cheese.

## About This Recipe

Anne-Mieke learned this recipe from her friend Richard Naranjo's father who was born in Mexico. He was a wonderful person and cook and his menudo was the best she ever tasted. Anne-Mieke grew up in Holland which has a tradition of heavy, potato- and bean-based food and when she moved to California she learned to love Mexican cuisine, which employs those same ingredients with much more flavor.
