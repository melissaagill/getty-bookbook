# Florentine Dill Pasta Salad

**Contributor:** Michael Moore

**Getty Entity:** Trust

**Department:** Security, Museum

---

## Servings & Ingredients

Serves 6 2 cups mostaccioli noodles 1/2 cup baby carrots, sliced 1/2 cup broccoli florets 1/4 cup butter 1/4 cup shallots, sliced 1 teaspoon crushed garlic 1 tablespoon fresh parsley, chopped 1 tablespoon fresh dill, chopped 1/2 cup water-packed artichoke hearts, quartered lengthwise 1/4 cup olive oil 1/3 cup rice vinegar 2 ozs. Parmesan cheese, grated 1/2 teaspoon paprika

---

## Instructions

Boil mostaccioli noodles until al dente; rinse in a colander with cool water and set aside. While the noodles are boiling, sautÃ© the carrots and broccoli in the butter and add to the mostaccioli. In a small mixing bowl combine the shallots, garlic, parsley, dill, artichoke hearts, oil and vinegar. Pour the contents of the mixing bowl over the mostaccioli, carrots and broccoli. Toss the mixture, coating the mostaccioli completely. Add the Parmesan cheese and toss again. Place in refrigerator to cool for half an hour. When ready to serve, place in shallow salad bowls or plates and garnish with paprika.

## About This Recipe

The creator of this dish is a former member of a public safety agency assigned in the Malibu area during several natural disasters occurring there. Following an enormous firestorm in 1993, a local community group published a fundraising cookbook to assist the victims of the disaster; this dish was among those selected. The Summer warmth and hospitality of both Malibu and Florence inspire its name. Malibu's Mediterranean type climate reminds Michael of Italy. As a cool summer dish, it is delicate with a fine array of spices and flavor. It is further inspired by the Getty's superb collection of Florentine art emphasizing color, texture and good taste.
