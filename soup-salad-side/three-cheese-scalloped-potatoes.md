# Three Cheese Scalloped Potatoes

**Contributor:** Carol Cressler

**Getty Entity:** Conservation Institute

**Department:** Information and Communications

---

## Servings & Ingredients

Serves 12 3/4 packed cup extra-sharp cheddar cheese, grated 3/4 cup Danish blue cheese, crumbled 1/3 cup Parmesan, freshly grated 4 lbs. russet potatoes, peeled and cut into 1/4-inch-thick rounds 1 1/2 teaspoons salt 1/2 teaspoon ground black pepper 1/4 cup onion, finely chopped 3 tablespoons all purpose flour 4 tablespoons (1/2 stick) butter 3 cups whole milk

---

## Instructions

Preheat oven to 400Â° F. Lightly butter baking dish. Mix cheddar cheese, blue cheese and Parmesan in small bowl. Arrange half of potatoes in prepared baking dish, overlapping slightly. Sprinkle with teaspoon salt and 1/4 teaspoon pepper. Sprinkle a layer of onions, then flour. Dot with 2 tablespoons butter. Sprinkle half of cheese mixture. Top with remaining potatoes, 3/4 teaspoon salt, 1/4 teaspoon pepper and 2 tablespoons butter. Reserve remaining cheese. Bring milk to simmer in medium saucepan. Pour milk over potatoes (milk will not cover potatoes completely). Cover baking dish tightly with foil. Bake 45 minutes. Uncover dish (liquids in dish may look curdled); sprinkle potatoes with reserved cheese mixture. Bake uncovered until potatoes are tender and cheese is deep golden brown, about 45 minutes longer. (Can be prepared 2 hours ahead. Let stand at room temperature. Cover and rewarm in 375Â° oven about 20 minutes.) Remove from oven; let stand 15 minutes before serving.

## About This Recipe

Carol likes to try new recipes and made this dish for a Fall potluck. She considers her co-workers her favorite guinea pigs! This particular dish was a big hit and there was not one morsel left. Carol says it is the blue cheese that makes the difference; even those who wrinkle their noses at blue cheese will love it.
