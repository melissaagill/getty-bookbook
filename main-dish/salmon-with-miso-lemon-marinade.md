# Salmon with Miso-Lemon Marinade

**Contributor:** Mikka Conway

**Getty Entity:** Museum

**Department:** Photographs Curatorial

---

## Servings & Ingredients

Serves 2 2 nice salmon steaks, about 3/4 lb. each or bigger (you will have leftovers) juice of 1 lemon (approximately 3 tablespoons), more if needed 2-3 tablespoons miso paste

---

## Instructions

Rinse the salmon steaks in cold water, removing any pin bones, and pat dry. Place in a shallow dish. Mix lemon juice and miso paste together until they form a smooth, thin paste. Add more lemon juice if necessary, this is not very scientific. You want it thin enough to spread easily over the salmon, but thick enough that it will cling a little bit. Spoon this over the salmon steaks, turning them over in the marinade, and let them sit for 5 to 45 minutes. Meanwhile, prepare a charcoal grill, or preheat the broiler. Grill or broil the salmon steaks about 5 minutes per side, depending on the thickness of the steaks This dish is great served with rice and sautÃ©ed or steamed Napa cabbage.

## About This Recipe

Mikka, who loves salmon, adapted this simple recipe from one of her mother (in which mirin, or Japanese rice wine, is substituted for lemon juice) when she found herself with some lovely salmon steaks and no mirin. The marinade can be thickened by varying the ratio of juice to miso, forming a nice crust on the fish when you broil it. This marinade also goes well with tuna or swordfish steaks.
