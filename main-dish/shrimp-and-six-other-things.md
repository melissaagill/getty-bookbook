# Shrimp and Six other things

**Contributor:** Wendy Sumida

**Getty Entity:** Research Institute

**Department:** Administration

---

## Servings & Ingredients

Serves 2-4 1 tablespoon ginger juice (the bottled kind) 1 tablespoon cilantro oil juice of one lemon 3 scallions, sliced into 1/4" crosswise pieces 1 tablespoon olive oil 12 large shrimp 1 teaspoon sea salt (if you do not have sea salt, add 1/4 teaspoon regular table salt, but you should really try to use sea salt)

---

## Instructions

This recipe is easy and goes very quickly so have everything ready. Put the first 4 ingredients in a small bowl and set aside. Remove shells, clean, de-vein, and butterfly the shrimp. Using a very sharp knife make several cuts in the flattened shrimp to keep it from curling up too much. Heat a skillet, wok or frying pan. When pan is hot, add olive oil, turning to coat bottom of pan; add shrimp. Cook on one side for about 30 seconds. Immediately add the bowl of ingredients and turn shrimp over to cook other side in the liquid. Sprinkle sea salt over the shrimp. Once shrimp turn pink (after cooking about another minute) remove and place in a serving dish, leaving the juices in the pan. Do not overcook the shrimp or you will have pink rubber in a yummy sauce. Reduce the juices for another minute or two and pour over shrimp. Serve hot.

## About This Recipe

This recipe was an experiment. After an afternoon of looking at equipment and ingredients at a local cooking supply store, Wendy tried think up a dish that used most of the things she had just bought. She was able to use 3 new ingredients in this dish.
