# Stuffed Peppers with Couscous and Artichoke

**Contributor:** Gene Karraker

**Getty Entity:** Museum

**Department:** Paintings Conservation

---

## Servings & Ingredients

Serves 4 1 lemon, halved 1 cup couscous salt and pepper 2 tablespoons unsalted butter, cut into small pieces 3 tablespoons olive oil, plus enough to coat the peppers 1/2 cup Nicoise olives, halved and pitted 3/4 cup Parmigiano-Reggiano cheese, freshly grated 24 baby artichokes (3 8 1/2-oz. cans of artichoke hearts can be substituted) 1 cup chicken stock 2 shallots, thinly sliced 1/4 lb. prosciutto, thinly sliced and chopped 1/2 cup pine nuts, toasted 8 medium-sized yellow peppers

---

## Instructions

Preheat oven to 400Â° F. (If canned artichokes are being used, the following can be disregarded.) Squeeze lemons into a large bowl of cold water. Cut off the artichoke stems. Trim the thorns and pull off the tough outer leaves. Half artichokes lengthwise, then thinly slice. Drop artichokes into the acidulated water. Put couscous in a heatproof bowl, season with salt and pepper and scatter the butter all over. In a small saucepan, bring stock to a boil and stir it into the couscous; cover and set aside for about 8 minutes. Fluff couscous with a fork; it should be soft. If not, add a little hot water. Meanwhile, in a large non-reactive skillet, heat 3 tablespoons oil. Drain artichokes. Add them to the skillet with shallots, season with salt and pepper and cook over moderate heat, stirring until the artichokes are beginning to brown, about 8 minutes. Reduce heat to moderately low, add 1/2 cup water and cook until tender, about 4 minutes (this step can be skipped if canned artichokes are being used). Add artichokes to couscous and let cool slightly. Add prosciutto, olives, pine nuts and half the cheese to the couscous. Season with salt and pepper and toss gently. Cut off the tops of the peppers and scoop out the seeds and ribs. Lightly brush peppers with olive oil and fill them with the couscous mixture. Sprinkle the remaining cheese on the top. Stand the peppers upright in a 13" x 9" baking dish. Pour about 1/2" of water into the dish and cover the peppers loosely with aluminum foil. Bake for 30 minutes, or until the peppers are barely tender. Remove the foil, set the reserved tops on the peppers and back for another 30 minutes or until the peppers are tender. Serve warm or at room temperature. To make ahead, peppers can be refrigerated, covered, overnight. Return to room temperature before reheating, covered with foil, in a 325Â° F oven.
