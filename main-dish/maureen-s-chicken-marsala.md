# Maureen's Chicken Marsala

**Contributor:** Betty Schodt

**Getty Entity:** Trust

**Department:** Accounting

---

## Servings & Ingredients

Serves 6 1 teaspoon canola oil 3/4 lb. skinless boneless chicken breasts, cut into strips 4 shallots, chopped 1 tablespoon all-purpose flour 1/2 cup low-sodium chicken broth 1/4 cup dry Marsala wine 1 cup seedless green grapes, halved 1/4 cup minced parsley 1/4 freshly ground black pepper 4 cups cooked brown or white rice

---

## Instructions

In a large nonstick skillet, heat the oil. Add the chicken and cook, turning as needed, until lightly browned, 5-6 minutes. Transfer to plate. Spray the same skillet with nonstick cooking spray; add shallots and cook, stirring as needed, until softened, about 5 minutes. Add flour stirring constantly, until the flour coats shallots, about 1 minute. Gradually stir in the broth and wine, still stirring, until the mixture boils and thickens, about 2 minutes. Stir in chicken, grapes, parsley and pepper and bring to a boil. Reduce heat and simmer, covered, until cooked through, about 5 minutes. Serve over a bed of rice.
