# Meatballs with Tomato Sauce

**Contributor:** John Palombi

**Getty Entity:** Trust

**Department:** Security

---

## Servings & Ingredients

Serves 15 Tomato Sauce: 1 28-oz. can crushed tomato 1 28-oz. can tomato purÃ©e 1 6-oz. can tomato paste 1 tablespoon sugar 3 tablespoons salt dash of pepper pinch of basil pinch of thyme 3 garlic cloves, chopped Meatballs: 3 lbs. ground beef 2 extra large eggs 1/2 cup breadcrumbs (more if needed) salt and pepper 1/4 cup Parmesan cheese, grated

---

## Instructions

Preheat oven to 350Â° F. In a large bowl mix the meatball ingredients until evenly blended. Lightly oil a baking pan. Roll the mixture into balls and place them on the oiled baking pan. Bake for 15 minutes, remove the pan from the oven and turn over the meatballs. Continue baking for another 15 minutes. Remove from the oven and set aside to cool. In a large pot combine all the ingredients for the sauce. Using a long-handled spoon stir until all the ingredients are evenly blended. Cook over moderately high heat until a slow boil is reached, stirring occasionally. Reduce to a very slow simmer. With a slotted spoon add the meatballs one at a time. Continue simmering for 3-4 hours, stirring occasionally to prevent the bottom from sticking.
