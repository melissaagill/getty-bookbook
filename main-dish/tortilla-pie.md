# Tortilla Pie

**Contributor:** Suzanne Winterhawk

**Getty Entity:** Museum

**Department:** Museum Info. & Media Systems

---

## Servings & Ingredients

Serves 4 1 can beans (black, refried, chili - chef's choice) 1 small can sliced black olives tortillas (either corn or flour) salsa 5-8 ozs. cheddar cheese, shredded

---

## Instructions

Spray casserole dish with nonstick spray. Place first tortilla flat in dish and spread with a fairly thick layer of beans. Sprinkle several tablespoons of olives, salsa and grated cheese over the beans. Add another tortilla and repeat layers until you fill the dish. End with a tortilla on top and sprinkle lightly with salsa and shredded cheese. Bake in the oven at 375Â° F for 45 minutes or microwave until cheese is melted and center is hot. Slice in wedges like a pie.


---

## About This Recipe

This is an easy and versatile recipe that can be dressed up with garnishes (sour cream, avocado, tomato slices, fresh cilantro sprigs.) Using flour tortillas makes it look and taste substantially different than the recipe with corn tortillas. Different kinds of canned beans, also, change the texture and flavor. This is an easy meal to make while camping or boating because it does not use many ingredients and no refrigeration is necessary. This meal can be made in heavy duty foil over a fire -- but turn frequently so that it does not burn.
