# Stuffed Tomatoes

**Contributor:** Jeannine Matzen

**Getty Entity:** Research Institute

**Department:** Provenance Index

---

## Servings & Ingredients

Serves 4 8 large tomatoes 8 ozs. ground veal (or mixture of 1/2 ground veal, 1/2 pork and beef) 4 slices of hard toast, crushed into crumbs 1 extra large egg, beaten 1 clove garlic, chopped 1/4 cup onion, chopped 3 tablespoons parsley, chopped salt black pepper, freshly ground olive oil

---

## Instructions

Preheat oven to 375Â° F. Cut the top off each tomato and set aside. Scoop pulp and seeds out; save pulp but discard seeds. Sprinkle tomatoes with salt and turn upside down on paper towel. Leave for 1/2 hour to drain. Mix meat with egg, garlic, onion, and parsley. Chop 1/4 of the tomato pulp and add to mixture; add toast crumbs; add salt and pepper to taste. Stuff tomatoes with meat mixture and replace tomato tops. Place tomatoes in a deep, ovenproof dish; put a drop of oil on each. Bake for 45 to 60 minutes. Bon Appetit!


---

## About This Recipe

This is a typical family French dish that is very tasty when tomatoes are in season. Jeannine's mother used to make a big casserole as it is also good cold. It becomes a complete meal when serve on a bed of steamed rice. Chopped spinach or chard can be substituted for parsley.
