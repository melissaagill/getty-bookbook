# Meatball and Cabbage Hoagie

**Contributor:** Stacy Perkins-Smith

**Getty Entity:** Museum

**Department:** Visitor Services

## Servings & Ingredients

Serves 6-8 Meatballs: 3 lbs. lean ground beef 1 medium-sized onion 3 extra large eggs 1/2 cup seasoned breadcrumbs 1 large garlic clove, chopped 1 teaspoon dried oregano 1 teaspoon Worshestershire sauce salt and pepper 1/4 cup extra virgin olive oil 1 large jar Prego pepperoni flavored spaghetti sauce 8 long french rolls Cabbage Topper: 2 cups green bell pepper, julienned 2 cups white onion, julienned 1 small head white cabbage, shredded 3 scallions, diced 1/4 cup sun-dried tomatoes, chopped 1/4 cup Parmesan cheese, grated 2 tablespoons olive oil 1 large garlic clove, chopped

---

## Instructions

To make meatballs, mix all meatball ingredients in a large bowl with the exception of the olive oil and spaghetti sauce. Heat olive oil in deep frying pan to 325Â° F. Keeping hands oiled, form meat mixture into 2" meatballs and fry in oil until well browned and cooked through. Turn meatballs continuously to keep nice round form. Once meatballs are completely cooked, heat spaghetti sauce in large sauce pan. Place meatballs in sauce and heat through. For cabbage topper, in large skillet or wok, heat olive oil to 300Â° F and begin to sautÃ© peppers and onion. Stir-fry until onions are translucent. Add sun-dried tomatoes, scallions and garlic. Cook until scallions are limp. Add cabbage and heat through. Add Parmesan and sautÃ© until melted. Briefly warm french roll in oven. Place meatballs end to end on roll (approx. 4-5) and top with 1/4 cup of cabbage mix.

## About This Recipe

Stacy grew up in a racially diverse family. Her mother, father, and older sister are in the restaurant or other type of food preparation business.
