# Roast Chicken with Rosemary and Garlic

**Contributor:** Karol Wight

**Getty Entity:** Museum

**Department:** Antiquities Curatorial

---

## Servings & Ingredients

Serves 4-6 1 whole chicken 2 tablespoons garlic, minced (or more if you are a garlic lover) fresh rosemary olive oil

---

## Instructions

Preheat oven to 350Â° F. Prepare chicken for roasting by emptying all body cavities, washing it in water and toweling it dry. Using your hands, separate breast and thigh skin from the meat below, and rub the minced garlic and rosemary into the cavities below the skin. Rub the minced garlic and rosemary inside the body and neck cavities as well. Rub the exterior of the chicken skin with olive oil. Place in a roasting pan on a rack and bake for one hour or until chicken is completely roasted. Baste chicken skin with drippings every 15 minutes. If there are no drippings initially, put more olive oil on the skin. After removing chicken from the oven, carve and serve with the skin on.

## About This Recipe

This chicken dish is a variation of a recipe found in Marcella Hazan's Italian cookbook and is a standby for Karol. It is easy to make and anyone who likes chicken loves the dish. He made it while on an archaeological excavation on Cyprus, and the kitchen help thought he was nuts when he requested some fresh rosemary as they never use this herb in their cooking. They found some at the local cemetery, where it had been planted in remembrance of the dead.
