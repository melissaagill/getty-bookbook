# Crock-Pot: German Short Ribs

**Contributor:** Sherry Creighton

**Getty Entity:** Trust

**Department:** Administration

---

## Servings & Ingredients

Serves 4 3 1/2 lbs. beef short ribs 2 tablespoons flour 1 teaspooon salt 1/2 teaspoon pepper 2 tablespoons shortening or Pam 2 medium-sized onions, sliced 1/2 cup dry red wine 1/2 cup chili sauce 3 tablespoons brown sugar 3 tablespoons vinegar 1 tablespoon Worcestershire sauce 1/2 teaspoon dry mustard 1/2 teaspoon chili powder

---

## Instructions

Coat short ribs in mixture of flour with salt and pepper. Melt shortening in large skillet. Add ribs and brown on all sides. Pour off excess fat. In crockpot, combine onions, wine, chili sauce, brown sugar, vinegar, Worcestershire sauce, mustard and chili powder. Cover and cook on low for 6 to 8 hours. Turn control to high. Thicken with flour that has been dissolved in a small amount of water. Cook on high for about 10 minutes or until slightly thickened. Serve over noodles.


---

## About This Recipe

This dish is fast and delicious and gets raves whenever it is made. Best on a cold, wintery day. Serve on noodles with parsley sprinkled over.
