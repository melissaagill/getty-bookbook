# Cuban Black Beans

**Contributor:** Marta de la Torre

**Getty Entity:** Conservation Institute

**Department:** Information and Communications

---

## Servings & Ingredients

Serves 6-8 1 lb. dry black beans 10 cups water 1 cup olive oil 2 green peppers 2 large onions 6 cloves garlic 1/2 tablespoon oregano 1/2 tablespoon ground cumin 1 bay leaf 4 teaspoons salt 1/2 teaspoon black pepper 2 tablespoons vinegar 1 tablespoon Worcestershire sauce 1/4 cup dry wine (red or white) 1/4 cup olive oil

---

## Instructions

Rinse beans well. Bring beans to a boil in water, and cook slowly until they are soft, approximately 2 to 3 hours (Soaking the beans for several hours or overnight prior to cooking will reduce cooking time to about 45 minutes.) Beans should be covered in liquid throughout the cooking time until they are soft; if more water is needed, it must be hot water. In a frying pan, sautÃ© diced onions, green pepper and garlic in olive oil for 15 minutes over low fire; add to beans. Add oregano, cumin, bay leaf, salt and pepper. Cook for approximately one hour in a soft boil, stirring often. Add vinegar, Worcestershire sauce, wine and olive oil, and cook over low fire another hour or until liquid has thickened. They can be prepared ahead of time. Cuban folklore says black beans taste better if they are refrigerated overnight after cooking. They can be frozen. Serve with white rice. If put through a blender and thinned with water, it can also be served as a soup.

## About This Recipe

This is a traditional Cuban way of cooking black beans. These beans, served with rice, are a staple of the Cuban diet. They are, however, also a holiday dish when the black beans and rice are served with roasted pork and yuca (cassava) in a garlic sauce.
