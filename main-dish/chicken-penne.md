# Chicken Penne

**Contributor:** Christina Donnelly

**Getty Entity:** Museum

**Department:** Visitor Services

---

## Servings & Ingredients

Serves 4 extra virgin olive oil fresh garlic salt and pepper fresh basil, chopped (not too chopped) Parmesan cheese, grated juice of 1 lemon white wine about 6 Roma tomatoes 2 lbs. boneless, skinless chicken breast chunks penne pasta

---

## Instructions

Measurements are all to taste. In wok-style skillet, heat olive oil and garlic over low heat. Add chicken and cook for 8-10 minutes, stirring occasionally. If the chunks are not too big, the chicken will cook relatively fast. You do not want to cook them completely yet. Add wine and lemon juice to taste. The wine, lemon juice and olive oil make up the sauce. You might want to add more olive oil if it cooked away with the chicken. Salt & pepper to taste. Add chunked tomatoes for about 5 minutes. Try not to let the tomatoes cook so much that they fall apart; they should remain pretty firm. Add cooked penne & Parmesan and toss. At the last moment sprinkle the fresh basil on top of each plate as you serve, otherwise the basil will wilt. Use a lot of basil because it is the most fabulous herb on the face of God's green earth.

## About This Recipe

Christina loves to cook fresh quick food. No slaving away in the kitchen for her. This is a super quick meal... start to finish, 20 minutes. It is light and fresh but still so yummy! You can substitute shrimp for the chicken and cappelini for the penne, that works nicely too. She stole the recipe from the head chef of a restaurant she worked in years ago. (Actually, it was a fair trade. He gave her Chicken Penne, and she showed him how to make the world's best Margarita.) It is great with Caesar salad and some beautiful crusty bread to dip in olive oil. She recommends a nice Pinot Grigio, both while you're cooking and when you sit down to enjoy this delicious, healthful dish with friends. You cannot go wrong. Enjoy!
