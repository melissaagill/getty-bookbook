## Servings & Ingredients

Serves 6 1 1/2 lbs. shrimp, peeled 1/2 large onion, chopped l green bell pepper, chopped 2-3 cloves garlic, minced 2-3 tablespoons butter, melted 2 tablespoons flour 2 tablespoons fresh parsley, chopped 1/2 teaspoon thyme leaves 1/2 teaspoons chili powder 2 bay leaves, crushed dash ground cloves dash cayenne pepper 3 cups fish stock or chicken bouillon 1 cup uncooked rice 1 teaspoon salt

## About This Recipe

This is a recipe from Marianne's son-in-law's grandfather who is now in his 90s. Jambalaya has become their traditional Christmas fare and a family favorite. Pere is turning 99 in August. He was raised in New Orleans and grew up loving to cook as a boy, spending many days with his mother and unmarried aunts in their hot, unairconditioned kitchen. Red beans and rice with hot sauce, thick jambalaya, spicy gazpacho, silky Hollandaise sauce and creamy pralines: all Pere's long life he has loved to cook and taught his children and most of his fourteen grandchildren and eleven great-grandchildren to cook, not only because it tastes so good, but also because the act of creating great food is so much a part of close family life. Marianne feels fortunate that her daughter married into this rich cooking tradition and that her son-in-law has inherited his grandfather's cooking skills and shares his wonderful recipes with all of us.
