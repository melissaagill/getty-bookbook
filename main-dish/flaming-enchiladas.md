# Flaming Enchiladas

**Contributor:** Elizabeth Escamilla

**Getty Entity:** Museum

**Department:** Education

## Servings & Ingredients

Yields 2 dozen enchiladas Sauce: 1 can cream of mushroom soup 1 can cream of chicken soup 10-12 boiled tomatillos, with 1/4 cup liquid 1-2 jalapeÃ±o peppers, roasted 2-4 California peppers, roasted 2 serrano peppers (only if you like very spicy food), roasted 2 red tomatoes, roasted 1 bunch cilantro 4-5 whole garlic cloves, peeled 1 yellow onion, chopped Enchilada filling: 4 boiled chicken breasts (preferably with bone), retain broth for enchilada sauce. Once chicken is cool enough to touch, shred into bowl. 6 rose potatoes, diced 1 small block of Monterey Jack cheese, shredded 2 dozen corn tortillas

---

## Instructions

To prepare sauce, place all sauce ingredients into a blender, and blend on high until smooth. Transfer to a pot and heat to a low boil, adding leftover chicken broth until desired consistency is reached.  Heat corn tortillas until soft (either directly on gas flame, in a nonstick pan, or in microwave oven), fry each tortilla in oil until lightly crisp. Quickly dunk each corn tortilla in hot sauce. Remove and place on plate; fill with chicken and diced potatoes. Roll tortilla and layer onto a cookie sheet. Once cookie sheet is full, pour more sauce over them and top with cheese. Place toothpicks into enchiladas and cover with foil. Bake at 350Â° for 40-50 minutes. If you have extra sauce, dribble it over enchiladas as you serve them to your guests.

## About This Recipe

Over 10 years ago, a college friend shared this recipe with Elizabeth. Since then, Flaming Enchiladas have been enjoyed by many friends during long discussions about life, love and happiness. Like all good things, the enchiladas get better with time -- the leftovers are almost better than the fresh ones. However, you will probably not have many leftovers. Variations: Instead of stuffing and rolling each enchilada (which can be very time consuming), layer tortillas flat along a casserole dish and alternate with layers of chicken, potatoes, tortillas, and cheese. After baking for 50 minutes at 350Â°, cut into squares and serve with extra sauce. Mexican rice is a nice compliment; a big summer salad with a light vinaigrette is also very good. Serve with cold beer or a robust red wine.
