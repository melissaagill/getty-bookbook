# Biriyani

**Contributor:** Wasima Khan

**Getty Entity:** Trust

**Department:** Information Technology Services, Administrative Services

## Servings & Ingredients

Serves 6 1 1/2-2 lbs. lamb shoulder & leg, cut into cubes (variations discussed above) 1/2 cup plain yogurt 4 bay leaves 4-6 cardamom seeds 6-8 whole cloves 1 stick cinnamon, broken into small bits 1 teaspoon fresh cumin powder 1 teaspoon red pepper, crushed 1 teaspoon paprika 1 teaspoon fresh ginger paste 1 teaspoon fresh garlic paste salt 2 medium-sized onions, finely sliced into circles 2 tablespoons ghee (clarified butter) or margarine pinch of saffron 8-10 small potatoes, peeled and cut in half 2 large cups basmati rice (approx. 1 lb.) 1 tablespoon slivered almonds 1 teaspoon raisins 1/2 cup coriander leaves (cilantro), coarsely chopped for garnish 2-3 cups hot water

---

## Instructions

In a large bowl, mix lamb, yogurt, bay leaves, cardamom, cloves, cinnamon, cumin, red pepper, paprika, ginger, garlic and salt. Marinate in refrigerator for 1 hour. Coat the bottom of a stainless steel or nonstick large pan lightly with oil or ghee and cook meat and spices covered on medium heat. Stir after 1/2 hour. Place saffron in a bowl of lukewarm water. Soak potato halves in water for a few minutes. Add to the pan. Stir and cover. Save saffron water for later use. When ingredients in pot are fairly dry and partially cooked, add about 1/2 cup water. Stir and cover. Wash and drain rice in colander. Let dry. In frying pan or skillet, heat ghee. Brown one of the sliced onions. Add in rice, almonds, raisins and salt. SautÃ© rice for a few minutes. Add 1 cup of hot water and lower heat. Cover and stir occasionally until rice is cooked (approximately 20-30 minutes). Add rice to the large pan and mix evenly with meat and potatoes. Pour saffron water over meat and rice for fragrance and let the ingredients cook together for 5 minutes. Serve on large platter or bowl. Garnish with coriander leaves and the other onion, sautÃ©ed until brown and crispy.

## About This Recipe

Mughlai cuisine comes from the region of North India and this elaborate Mughlai dish is fit for royalty. Rich, spicy and aromatic, Biryani is traditionally served at weddings and special occasions. There are numerous variations of Biriyani. This particular recipe comes from Wasima's observations of her mother and aunt in the kitchen. Instead of lamb, one can substitute 2 Cornish game hens, small chickens, or mixed vegetables (cauliflower, beans, carrots, tomatoes, potatoes). The other ingredients and cooking process will be the same, but cooking time will be shorter for just vegetables.
