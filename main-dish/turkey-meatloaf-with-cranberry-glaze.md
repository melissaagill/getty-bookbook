# Turkey Meatloaf with Cranberry Glaze

**Contributor:** Sandy Silver

**Getty Entity:** Conservation Institute

**Department:** Administration

## Servings & Ingredients

Serves 6 1/4 cup brown sugar 1/2 cup mashed cranberry sauce 2 lbs. ground turkey 3/4 cup milk 3/4 cup breadcrumbs 3 extra large eggs, lightly beaten 1 1/2 teaspoons salt 1/8 teaspoon pepper 1/4 cup red onions, diced 1/2 cup chicken broth (just enough to moisten)

---
