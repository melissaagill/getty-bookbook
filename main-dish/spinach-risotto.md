# Spinach Risotto

**Contributor:** Stephanie Karos

**Getty Entity:** Trust

**Department:** Events

---

## Servings & Ingredients

Serves 2 1 10-oz. package chopped frozen spinach 3 cups chicken broth 1 cup water 1 cup onion, finely chopped 2 tablespoons olive oil 1 cup long grain rice or Arborio rice 1/3 cup dry white wine or dry vermouth 1 teaspoon Pernod (optional) 1/2 cup Parmesan cheese, freshly grated

---

## Instructions

Cook spinach according to package instructions and drain well. Cool spinach completely and squeeze dry by handfuls. In a saucepan bring broth and water to a low simmer. In a large saucepan cook onion in oil over moderately low heat, stirring often until softened. Stir in rice, stirring until each grain is coated with oil. Add wine or vermouth and cook covered over moderately high heat, stirring until wine is absorbed. Add about 3/4 cup simmering broth and cook over moderately high heat, stirring constantly until broth is absorbed. Continue adding broth, about 3/4 cup at a time, cooking, stirring constantly and letting each addition be absorbed before adding the next, until half of broth has been added. Reduce heat to moderate if necessary to keep risotto at a strong simmer. Continue adding the broth in the same manner until rice is tender, creamy looking but still al dente, about 18 minutes. Stir spinach (and Pernod if desired) into rice and add salt and pepper to taste. Remove pan from heat and stir in 1/4 cup Parmesan cheese. Serve risotto with remaining 1/4 cup Parmesan.

## About This Recipe

This spinach risotto is great for a very small dinner with some good crusty bread, a wonderful wine, and some classical music in the background. Definitely worth all the stirring. Fabuloso!
