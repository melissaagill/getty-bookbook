# Chicken Fajitas

**Contributor:** Rosie Urrutia and Wayne Branham

**Getty Entity:** Facilities and Villa Engineering

**Department:** nan

---

## Servings & Ingredients

Variable chicken breasts onion green, red and yellow bell peppers tomato Lawry's Taco Seasoning oil salt and pepper

---

## Instructions

Boil chicken breasts. Slice chicken in long strips. Cook chicken in skillet with oil and taco seasoning. Slice vegetables in strips and add to chicken. Serve inside flour tortillas.
