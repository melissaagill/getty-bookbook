# Asparagus Frittata or What to do with leftover pasta?

**Contributor:** Rebecca Bubenas

**Getty Entity:** Trust

**Department:** Communications

## Servings & Ingredients

Yields 8 wedges as appetizer, 4 if main course Frittata: 1 teaspoon olive oil 1/4 pound ham, diced 6 extra large eggs 3 tablespoons Parmesan cheese, grated pinch salt few grindings of pepper 1/4 cup fresh basil or parsley, chopped 2 cups cooked spaghetti (room temperature or cold, but not hot) 1/2 cup mozzarella or brie cheese, cubed 10 spears thin asparagus (or 5 thick spears, sliced lengthwise) 2 tablespoons olive oil 2 tablespoons butter Fresh tomato purÃ©e: 4 ripe homegrown tomatoes 1 large clove garlic, peeled and crushed 1 tablespoon olive oil 1 teaspoon coarse sea salt 4 leaves fresh Italian or lettuce-leaf basil

## Instructions

Using a 9-inch frying pan, sautÃ© ham in 1 teaspoon of olive oil until crispy. Drain on paper. Clean and trim asparagus. Cook in boiling water until tender. Drain and set aside. In a large bowl, beat eggs lightly with parmesan, salt, pepper, and herbs. Stir in the spaghetti and blend well. Add in the cubed cheese and ham. In the frying pan, heat butter and olive oil. Place the asparagus spears around the bottom of the pan, in any arrangement that appeals to you. Gently add the spaghetti mixture--smooth out the top carefully, without moving the asparagus. Cook on the stove-top at medium-low heat until golden brown on the bottom and slightly firm on top. Remove the pan from the flame. Place a plate (one that s larger than the pan) over the top of the pan. Flip over to release the frittata onto the plate. Add butter and oil to pan, put the pan back on low heat. When the butter has just melted, slide frittata back into the pan to cook the other side (the asparagus will now be on top.) Shake the pan a little during this final cooking so the frittata does not stick. Slide the finished frittata onto a serving plate. Cut into wedges. To make tomato purÃ©e, dip tomatoes in boiling water for 15 seconds. Remove from water. Peel off skins. Remove seeds. Mash pulp and liquid in a bowl, or purÃ©e in a blender. Add garlic, olive oil, and salt. Blend well. Adjust seasoning to taste. Scrape into a serving bowl. Chop the basil leaves into a fine chiffonade. Sprinkle on top of the purÃ©e. Makes about 1/2- 3/4 cup, depending on the size of the tomatoes.
