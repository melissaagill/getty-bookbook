# Greek Turkey Tacos with Dill Caraway Tortillas

**Contributor:** Terri Buzzard

**Getty Entity:** Bon Appetit

**Department:** nan

---

## Servings & Ingredients

Serves 4 Dill Caraway Tortilla Ingredients: 4 cups all purpose flour 1/2 teaspoon rapid rising yeast 1/2 teaspoon baking powder 1 cup plain yogurt 1/2 cup buttermilk 1 tablespoon dried dill 1 tablespoon caraway seeds, roasted water as needed salt Salsa ingredients: 2 cucumbers, peeled and seeded 1 red onion, diced 1/2 cup black olives, diced 2-3 Roma tomatoes, diced 1/2 cup fresh mint, chopped juice of 1 lemon salt Meat Ingredients: no. 1 ground turkey 1 white onion, diced 1 lemon, juiced and zested 1 tablespoon fresh oregano 2 cloves garlic, minced olive oil for cooking salt and pepper feta cheese, crumbled sour cream steamed jasmine rice

---

## Instructions

Prepare tortillas first: In a large bowl combine all dry ingredients, mix. Add yogurt, mix, then add buttermilk. This might be plenty of liquid but if not add water. The dough should be a little sticky and needs only to be mixed thoroughly not kneaded. Let sit about 30 minutes. Make balls the size of golfballs, roll out and place on parchment or butcher paper. Refrigerate about 30 minutes. Cook on flat top or skillet about 30 seconds on each side or until done. Keep warm. Cucumber salsa: Peel and seed cucumbers and dice small. Place in medium-sized bowl with diced red onion, diced olive, diced tomato, chopped mint and juice of one lemon. Mix together well, salt to taste. Meat preparation: In a hot sautÃ© pan with olive oil, sautÃ© onion, garlic and lemon zest till lightly golden. Add ground turkey and cook till done, about five minutes. When done, add juice from lemon and fresh oregano. Season to taste. Assemble taco: tortilla, meat, sour cream, feta cheese and cucumber salsa. Serve with plain steamed rice.

## About This Recipe

Terri is our housechef in the Restaurant at the Getty, and created this dish when working at Sony music. Sony held a chef exchange every year and this dish was created at the University of California, Santa Barbara campus. The students loved it and so did she. The handmade tortillas make the dish special, and the flavors are fabulous. Terri enjoys cooking at the Getty, but there is something special about cooking for friends and family at home. This is a refreshing twist on regular tacos and Terry knows you will find it quite tasty.
