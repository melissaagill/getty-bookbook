# Villa Herb Garden Meatloaf

**Contributor:** Amy Noel

**Getty Entity:** Museum

**Department:** Collections Information Planning

---

## Servings & Ingredients

Serves 4 1-1 1/2 lbs. ground beef 1 large egg 1 large onion, chopped 1/2 cup breadcrumbs or crackers, crumbled fresh picked cilantro, chopped (for Mexican variety meatloaf) or fresh picked basil, chopped (for Italian varietyf meatloaf) garlic salt dash ground cumin

---

## Instructions

Mix all ingredients with love in a big bowl, shape into a loaf. Plop into a bread pan and bake in the oven at 350Â° F for about an hour. Melt slices of cheddar cheese on top during the last 15-30 minutes of baking. Slice and serve with fresh sliced tomatoes and something green.

## About This Recipe

Amy's first day at the Getty was January 16, 1984, the 10th anniversary of the opening of the Villa. She worked in the Registrar's Office in various capacities for nearly 15 years, becoming the Collections Information System Manager in 1987. In 1998 her position moved to the Collections Information Department. She has three children. Raiding the herb garden at the Villa for fresh herbs over the years has inspired and enriched her cooking.

## Getty Memories

When I think of community food and the Getty, I think of paintings of great feasts and flowers and basil. I think of quilt party luncheons, cinco de mayo (when it was an informal potluck affair) and the cake Amanda and Jerry Podany made to replicate the Villa. The herb garden and various nooks and crannies around the Villa were always full of basil come summer. There I learned to make pesto, and how wonderful things taste when you cook with herbs fresh from the garden. Store bought fresh is good, farmers market fresh even better, but nothing compares to fresh picked, after watching it grow.
