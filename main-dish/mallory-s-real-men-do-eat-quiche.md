## About This Recipe

This recipe was given to Lisa by her best friend in college (who had the same last name, hence the recipe's title). She has been serving it at dinner parties for friends ever since and always receives compliments on it. It is yummy served with a good salad and wine. You can substitute with lowfat cheeses and lowfat evaporated milk if you are worried about the calories. It is also great eaten cold the next day.
