# Seabass with Cilantro-Lime Sauce

**Contributor:** Kezia Schulhof

**Getty Entity:** Trust

**Department:** Administration

---

## Servings & Ingredients

2-4 servings 2 tablespoons lime juice 1 1/2 tablespoons soy sauce 1 1/2 tablespoons cilantro, chopped 1 tablespoon fresh ginger, peeled 1 tablespoon shallots, minced 4 tablespoons olive oil 2 4- to 6-oz. seabass pieces 8 medium-sized fresh shitake mushrooms (keep whole, do not slice) 1/4 lb. fresh snow peas sprigs of fresh cilantro for garnish

---

## Instructions

Adjust oven rack to lower middle level and preheat oven to 500Â° F. Add first six ingredients to food processor and blend. Season sauce with salt and pepper to taste. Thinly coat glass baking dish with olive oil. Place seabass in dish and coat well with sauce, turning over to coat each side. Keep enough sauce to spoon over dish upon serving. Baked uncovered until done, approximately 10-12 minutes, depending on thickness of fish. While baking fish, quickly sautÃ© or stir-fry whole shitake mushrooms and snow peas in small amount olive oil, salt and pepper. Drain on paper towels and keep warm before serving. Serving: On dinner plates arrange snow peas in lattice fashion and place four shitake mushrooms in a square arrangement on top. Arrange piece of seabass atop the vegetables and spoon remaining sauce over the compilation before serving. Garnish with sprig of cilantro.
