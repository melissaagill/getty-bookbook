# California Paella

**Contributor:** Megan Kissinger

**Getty Entity:** Museum

**Department:** Research Associate

---

## Servings & Ingredients

Serves 4-6 2-3 cloves garlic 2 tablespoons olive oil 2 onions, chopped 1 bell pepper, chopped 2-3 tomatoes, peeled and chopped 2 cups rice 4 1/2 cups chicken stock (heated to a simmer) saffron threads salt pepper 2 lemons, quartered parsley, chopped Options: chicken sausage (or chorizo) squid, cleaned and chopped shrimp fish shell fish

---

## Instructions

SautÃ© garlic cloves in olive oil for 1 minute over high heat. Add onions and sautÃ© over medium heat for another 10 minutes. Add bell pepper and sautÃ© for another few minutes. If using chicken, add chicken pieces (not too big) over medium-high heat. If using sausage or chorizo, add slices. Add tomatoes and cook over high heat. Add a fat pinch of saffron, preferably threads. Add salt and pepper to taste and simmer for 2-3 minutes. Add uncooked rice and stir for 30 seconds until coated. Add squid pieces, if being used. Add hot chicken stock, bring to a boil, cover and simmer over low heat for 20 minutes. Add shrimp, fish or shellfish, adding pieces 10 minutes into simmering. Sprinkle with parsley and serve with lemons.
