# Quick Lasagna

**Contributor:** Libby Rogers

**Getty Entity:** Museum

**Department:** Administration

---

## Servings & Ingredients

Serves 6 1 32-oz. jar tomato sauce 2 garlic cloves, chopped 3/4 cup dry red wine 1 1/4 cups water 1 teaspoon oregano 2 extra large eggs, beaten 2 cups part-skim ricotta cheese 1/4 cup Parmesan cheese, grated 1/4 teaspoon ground nutmeg 1 lb. lasagna noodles, uncooked 1 lb. mozzarella cheese, grated

---

## Instructions

In large bowl combine tomato sauce, garlic, wine, water and oregano. In another bowl combine eggs, ricotta, Parmesan and nutmeg. Pour a generous amount of sauce on the bottom of 9 x 13" pan. Alternating the lasagna, sauce, ricotta mixture and mozzarella, make about three layers, ending with the mozzarella. Cover the pan tightly with aluminum foil and bake 1 hour; remove foil and continue baking 15 minutes or until sauce has thickened and noodles are tender. Let sit 10 minutes before cutting.
