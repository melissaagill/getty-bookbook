# Pepper Pasta Chicken Gorgonzola

**Contributor:** Marilyn Gillette

**Getty Entity:** Trust

**Department:** Information Technology Services, Director's Office

---

## Servings & Ingredients

Serves 4 4 boneless chicken breasts with skin big bottle of teriyaki marinade 1 12-oz. package red bell pepper pasta (order from Decio Pasta 1-480-456-1080) 4 tablespoons butter or margarine 4 tablespoons flour 2 cups milk salt big wedge of Gorgonzola cheese (maybe 12 ozs. or so)

---

## Instructions

Cover and marinate chicken in teriyaki sauce all day in refrigerator. Make a white sauce by melting butter in medium-sized pan, add flour and stir until blended. Add milk and stir until thick. Add a little salt. Crumble the Gorgonzola cheese into white sauce and stir until blended. Get someone who knows how to barbecue to barbecue the chicken (this takes maybe 20 minutes, 10 minutes per side). Cook pasta according to package directions. Serve pasta with Gorgonzola sauce, topped with sliced chicken.


---

## About This Recipe

This dish requires no advanced cooking skills or fancy preparation. The combination of flavors may seem extreme, but they work well together. Good for children who may eat pasta and chicken, but not sauce, or chicken and sauce but not pasta, etc., while others get a hearty meal.
