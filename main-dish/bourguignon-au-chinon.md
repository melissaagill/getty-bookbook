# Bourguignon au Chinon

**Contributor:** Laurence Liotta

**Getty Entity:** Museum

**Department:** Bookstore

---

## Servings & Ingredients

Serves 4 2 lbs. braising beef, cut into small cubes 1/2 lb. unsalted bacon (optional) 1 large onion, chopped 2 shallots, chopped 2 tablespoons sifted flour 2 cups red wine, Chinon or Bourgueil 1 1/2 cups beef stock 2 teaspoon bouquet garni 2 bay leaves 2 cloves garlic, finely chopped 1 dozen small button mushrooms 1 bunch parsley, finely chopped salt and pepper butter olive oil

---

## Instructions

Heat 1 tablespoon olive oil with 1 tablespoon butter in a large cooking pot. Add the onion and shallots and cook over medium heat stirring constantly until slightly browned. With a wooden spoon, remove and set aside on a plate. Repeat with bacon, remove and set aside with the onion and shallots. Add more oil if necessary and brown meat on all sides. Return onion, shallots and bacon to pot and stir to mix with meat, then sprinkle in flour. Cook, stirring constantly for 1-2 minutes, then add wine and beef stock. Add the bouquet garni and bay leaves and let simmer for 2-2 1/2 hours or until meat is tender. In a pan, melt some butter and brown sliced mushrooms with garlic until golden. Add parsley, stir and add to beef stew. Let simmer for an additional 15 minutes. Serve with steamed small potatoes and toasted slices of bread rubbed with garlic cloves. By replacing the usual Burgundy wine with a lighter wine like Chinon or Bourgueil you add a more subtle taste.


---

## About This Recipe

This traditional dish is a part of Laurence's childhood. She has happy memories of winter visits with her parents to Versailles, France when her maternal grandmother would cook Bourguignon au Chinon. Bourguignon is also delicious served with fresh pasta and gains in flavor when reheated. By rubbing a garlic clove onto bread, you obtain the flavor of traditional garlic bread without adding butter.
