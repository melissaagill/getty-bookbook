# Spinach Lasagna Alfredo

**Contributor:** Danielle Davenport

**Getty Entity:** Trust

**Department:** Publications

---

## Servings & Ingredients

Serves 8-10 12 ozs. lasagna noodles, uncooked 1 lb. pork sausage (Jimmy Dean sausage is good) 1 10-oz. package frozen chopped spinach, thawed and well drained 1 17-oz. jar Alfredo sauce (5 Brothers Alfredo sauce works well) 1/2 teaspoon salt 1/4 teaspoon pepper 1 extra large egg, beaten 2 cups cheddar cheese, shredded 1 15-oz. package ricotta cheese 1/2 cup Parmesan cheese, grated 1 cup mozzarella cheese, shredded 1 can mushrooms (optional)

---

## Instructions

Cook the lasagna noodles in boiling water for 15 minutes. In a skillet, brown sausage. Drain sausage, and add spinach, Alfredo sauce, salt and pepper. In a bowl combine the egg and cheddar, ricotta and Parmesan cheeses. Mix well. In an ungreased baking dish layer a third of the sausage mixture, noodles and cheese mixture. Repeat layers twice. Sprinkle with mozzarella cheese. Cover and bake at 350Â° F for 45 min. Let stand for 5 minutes before cutting.
