# Barbecue Hamburgers

**Contributor:** Catherine Fritz

**Getty Entity:** Conservation Institute

**Department:** Administration

---

## Servings & Ingredients

Serves 4 1 lb. lean ground beef 1 medium-sized onion, chopped fine 1/2 cup catsup 1/2 cup water 1/2 cup chopped celery 1/4 cup pickle relish 1 teaspoon salt dash of pepper

---

## Instructions

Brown ground beef. After meat is browned, add onion, water, celery, pickle relish, salt and pepper. Cook slowly for 30 minutes. Serve on hamburger buns or onion rolls -- good when served open-faced.


---

## About This Recipe

Catherine's grandmother began cooking this recipe many years ago and it was given to her mom after she married Catherine's dad. This is a great summer dish with cold potato salad.
