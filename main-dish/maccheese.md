# MacCheese

**Contributor:** Anne Lyden

**Getty Entity:** Museum

**Department:** Photographs Curatorial

---

## Servings & Ingredients

Serves 3-4 1/2 lb. penne pasta 1 large tomato 1 small yellow onion 6 strips bacon 1 teaspoon Dijon mustard 1 cup Scottish cheddar cheese, grated (substitute any sharp cheddar) 8-10 slices Scottish cheddar cheese 3/4 cup milk 1 tablespoon butter 1 tablespoon all-purpose flour

---

## Instructions

Add pasta to boiling water and cook until al dente. In the meantime, broil bacon. Chop onion and half of tomato into small chunks and place in medium-sized casserole dish along with mustard. Once bacon is cooked, cut into small pieces and add to casserole dish. When pasta is ready, drain and add to casserole, ensuring that all ingredients are evenly tossed. For cheese sauce, melt butter in small saucepan, then add flour. Pour in all the milk at once and begin stirring until sauce thickens.  Add cheese and continue to stir until cheese is melted and sauce is smooth. Stir sauce through the casserole, ensuring pasta is evenly coated. Place thin slices of cheese on top of casserole, then cut the remaining half of tomato into thin slices and arrange on top of cheese. Place casserole (uncovered) under the broiler for 5-8 minutes, or until cheese is almost crispy. Serve immediately and season to taste.

## About This Recipe

Annie likes to cook but only if it is a simple recipe! She is particularly fond of this one, not only because it is simple and quick but it is also her mother's own recipe. Annie remembers coming home from school on cold winter days back in Scotland and sitting down with her family to eat this tasty dish her mum had prepared.
