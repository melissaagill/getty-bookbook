# Stroganoff

**Contributor:** Aimee Calfin

**Getty Entity:** Research Institute

**Department:** Collection Management

---

## Servings & Ingredients

Serves 4 2 lbs. rib eye steak, thinly sliced (preferably sukiyaki quality, or top sirloin) 3 large brown onions, cut up in 1/2 inch squares 1/2 lb. or more fresh white button mushrooms, sliced 3/4 stick butter 6 or more cloves fresh garlic, finely diced 1 cup mirin (either Kikkoman Manjo or Aji Mirin) 1 cup soy sauce 1 pint sour cream Pam canola oil black pepper garlic powder 1 lb.-bag wide egg noodles

---

## Instructions

Cook egg noodles, and set aside. Pam a large frying pan, then add butter. As butter is melting, add diced onions and garlic into the pan, cover and sautÃ© until ingredients are reduced to half their original volume. Add sliced mushrooms, soy sauce and mirin. Stir and simmer for another 3 to 4 minutes. As ingredients in the first pan are simmering, Pam another, smaller frying pan, add canola oil as required, and begin stir frying the meat, small portions at a time (not all at once). After each portion is properly stir fried, add to the mixture in larger pan, until all meat has been cooked and added in, then stir entire mixture at medium heat for another 4 minutes. Add black pepper and garlic powder to taste. Leave in pan, set finished stroganoff on table with a separate dish containing egg noodles. Top off with sour cream.
