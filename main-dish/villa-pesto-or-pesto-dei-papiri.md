# Villa Pesto or Pesto dei Papiri

**Contributor:** Elisabeth Mention

**Getty Entity:** Museum

**Department:** Paintings Conservation

---

## Servings & Ingredients

Serves 4 2 cups fresh basil leaves 2 cloves garlic, peeled 2 tablespoons pine nuts 6 tablespoons good quality Parmesan cheese 1/2 cup extra virgin olive oil

---

## Instructions

Place several tablespoons of the olive oil and garlic in food processor fitted with metal blade. Process until finely chopped. Add basil leaves, pine nuts and cheese, and with machine running add olive oil in a fine stream until incorporated. Do not overprocess; it should have some texture. Cook pasta of your choice until al dente, drain and immediately put several tablespoons of pesto on top, and serve. Tip: if you do not add the cheese, the pesto freezes beautifully. Put it in ice cube trays, then transfer the frozen cubes to Ziploc freezer bags. Each cube is perfect for one serving. The cheese can be added to the sauced pasta.


---

## About This Recipe

Elisabeth began working at the Getty shortly after the Villa opened in 1974. In those days the staff was small and they'd often make a communal lunch in the founder's room kitchen. Pesto was still exotic fare at that time and they felt very continental as they would harvest basil from the herb garden and then sit down to a special repast.
