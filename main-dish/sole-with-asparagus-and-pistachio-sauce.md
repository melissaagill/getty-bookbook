# Sole with Asparagus and Pistachio Sauce

**Contributor:** Jennifer Wulffson

**Getty Entity:** Research Institute

**Department:** Vocabulary Program

---

## Servings & Ingredients

Serves 4 1 cup heavy whipping cream 1 cup stock (fish or chicken) 5 ozs. pistachios, shelled 1/2 cup dry white wine 2 tablespoons butter, cut into pieces 1 1/2-2 lbs. sole fillets salt and pepper 1-2 tablespoons olive oil 1 bunch fresh asparagus 10 ozs. fettuccine (regular or spinach), cooked al dente

---

## About This Recipe

This is a variation of a recipe given to Jennifer by her great friends in Washington, D.C., Sarah Finlay and Patrick Murcia, who she met while there for graduate school. Sarah and Patrick always present food beautifully, and this recipe is no exception. You can vary the recipe by omitting the pasta, changing the sole for another white fish, or by changing or omitting the vegetable. It is a great recipe for a small dinner party.
