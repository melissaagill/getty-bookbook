# Pork Adobo

**Contributor:** Philip Wayne

**Getty Entity:** Trust

**Department:** Event Production Services

---

## Servings & Ingredients

Serves 12 2 lbs. pork (chicken or beef may be substituted) 2 tablespoons garlic, crushed 1 cup white vinegar 1/4 cup soy sauce 1/2 cup water 2 tablespoons salt 1/2 tablespoon pepper (optional) 1/2 cup cooking oil 1/2 teaspoon sugar (optional)

---

## Instructions

With a sharp kitchen knife trim fat from meat. Cut into rectangular pieces about 1 x 2 inches. In a large pot blend vinegar, soy sauce, and water. Stir in salt and meat. Bring to a boil over moderately high heat, reduce to a simmer and cover. After 5 minutes stir in garlic. Continue simmering, stirring occasionally to blend ingredients and prevent meat from sticking to the bottom. When vinegar and soy sauce start to dry in pan add oil and let sit over a slow fire for another 20 minutes. For extra taste stir in sugar and let sit another 5 minutes over low flame.


---

## About This Recipe

This dish is best made with pork, but beef and chicken are alternatives. Chicken cooks quickly so vary cooking times. Serve with slight sauce or cook it dry with each meat cubed. Eat with steamed rice or bread while drinking cold beer. It is also good in a sandwich. Try 1 1/4 cup of liquor such as brandy, whiskey or gin before serving. While different varieties of adobo are cooked in the Philippines, the basic recipe is simple.
