# Mediterranean Summer Pasta

**Contributor:** Robin Trento

**Getty Entity:** Museum

**Department:** Education

---

## Servings & Ingredients

Serves 4-6 3 large ripe tomatoes, seeded and diced 2 stalks celery, split lengthwise and chopped 1/3 onion, finely chopped 2-3 cloves garlic, minced 16 large Greek olives, pitted and halved 2 tablespoons capers 2 tablespoons oregano 1 tablespoon hot pepper flakes small bunch fresh basil leaves, torn in pieces 1 1/2 cups extra virgin olive oil 1 lb. spaghetti

---

## Instructions

Combine all ingredients (except spaghetti) into a deep bowl and allow the flavors to mingle and blend for about 1/2 hour, stirring occasionally. Bring a large pot of water to a boil, add some salt, and cook the spaghetti until it is al dente. Strain and combine at once with the raw vegetables, mixing well. Buon Appetito.


---

## About This Recipe

The success of this dish depends entirely on the quality of the ingredients used. Home-grown or vine-ripened tomatoes, in season, are a must; as is good quality olive oil, Greek olives in brine, and preferably capers cured in salt (not vinegar). Use a good Italian pasta, such as de Cecco. Amounts given are approximate and can be varied according to taste. This dish is delicious hot, served as a first or main course to a summer meal. But it can also be eaten room temperature. When made with shell or gnocchi shaped pasta it makes a great pasta salad to bring to a party or picnic. The smell and taste are the pure essence of a Mediterranean summer.
