# California Brown Rice Pudding

**Contributor:** Lorraine Forrest

**Getty Entity:** Museum

**Department:** Registrar

---

## Servings & Ingredients

Serves 6-8 2 extra large eggs 1/4 cup honey 2 cups milk, scalded 1 1/2 cups cooked, cooled brown rice 1 teaspoon vanilla dash ground cinnamon dash ground nutmeg

---

## Instructions

Beat eggs well with honey. Gradually beat in milk. Stir in rice, vanilla, spices. Pour into greased one-quart casserole dish. Set in shallow pan. Pour hot water into pan to 1" deep. Bake at 325Â° F for 1 1/2 hours. Cool. Cover and chill. Serve cold.

## About This Recipe

A frequently requested breakfast treat in the Forrest home, Lorraine and her family included this dish in a picnic enjoyed while watching the sunrise from beneath a Christo umbrella.
