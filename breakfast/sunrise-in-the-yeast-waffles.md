# Sunrise in the Yeast Waffles

## Servings & Ingredients

Yields about 6 8" waffles 2 1/4 cups unbleached all-purpose flour 3 tablespoons of sugar 2 1/4 teaspoons of dry active yeast (one package) 3/4 teaspoon salt 1 cup warm milk (105 - 115Â° F.) 1 cup buttermilk (105 - 115Â° F.) 3 extra large eggs, beaten 1/4 cup of butter, melted 2 teaspoons vanilla extract

---

## Instructions

In a large bowl mix all of the ingredients in the order listed until well blended (lumps in the batter are fine; it is best not to over mix). Be careful when heating the buttermilk; it curdles more quickly than milk. Using a microwave judiciously is a good way to heat the milk and melt the butter. Cover the batter and let rise for 45 minutes in a warm place without drafts (Tip: turn on the oven at its lowest temperature for about 10 seconds, turn it off, and then place the batter in it). Begin heating your waffle iron on high about 15 minutes before the batter is ready. Stir down the batter before using. I have an eight-inch round non-stick waffle iron. Without using any additional shortening on the iron, I put four small ladles of batter into the iron, which ends up being about the right amount to fill the iron. Cooking time is usually just a couple of minutes, depending on how dark you like your waffles. Serve with maple syrup and enjoy.

## About This Recipe

Steve has been a waffle fan since early childhood, but he is too lazy to beat the egg whites necessary to produce dreamy light waffles. "Sunrise in the Yeast Waffles" are light, fluffy, easy to make, and cannot be beat. This recipe recently inspired an Italian guest to purchase a waffle iron to take back to Italy. As Steve warned him, however, the life of a "waffelaio" is a lonely one, as the waffle chef is often left laboring alone while family and friends eat waffle after waffle.
