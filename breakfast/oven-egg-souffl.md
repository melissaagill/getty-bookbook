## Servings & Ingredients

Serves 8 6 extra large eggs 2 cups half-and-half or milk 12 slices sandwich bread (crust removed) 1/2 lb. (2 jars) Kraft Old English Cheese Spread 1/4 lb. butter or margarine (1 stick) 1 teaspoon salt 1/4 teaspoon dry mustard

---

## Instructions

In heavy pan, melt cheese and butter. Cube bread, spread in 2 quart, greased casserole dish. Pour cheese sauce over bread. Beat eggs well and add milk, salt, and mustard. Pour over bread and cheese. Mix slightly with fork. Let stand in refrigerator overnight. Take out 2 hours before baking and let stand at room temperature. If frozen, take out the night before and let stand at room temperature. Fluff with fork before baking. Bake at 300Â° F for 1 hour (1/2 hour covered, and last 1/2 hour uncovered). Freezes well (before and after cooking).

## About This Recipe

Rogene's mom started making this many years ago, generally around the holidays. It is an easy way to make breakfast the night before and all that is needed to do on the holiday morning (or when you have company) is to bake it. It gets requested everytime Rogene flies back home to Wisconsin. Nothing beats mom's cooking real comfort food!
