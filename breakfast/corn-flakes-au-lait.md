## About This Recipe

Sheri is a single gal who does not cook often. She has found this to be a quick and easy recipe that always brings back happy memories from childhood. In terms of variations, one can use a different type of cereal, or soy milk instead of cow milk. Or yogurt instead of milk. But the best memories are evoked when one uses cow milk. And, technically, if one uses soy milk or yogurt, the French might not approve of the "au lait" appellation. In terms of presentation, while this dish can be served in a number of different styles, it is most fun when served using your best china.
