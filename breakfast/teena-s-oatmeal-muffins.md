# Teena's Oatmeal Muffins

**Contributor:** Ann Schneider

**Getty Entity:** Grant Program

**Department:** Administration

---

## Servings & Ingredients

Yields 1 dozen muffins 1 cup whole or quick cooking oats 1 cup buttermilk 1 extra large egg 1/2 cup melted shortening, cooled to room temperature 1/2 cup brown sugar 1 cup sifted all-purpose flour 1 teaspoon baking powder 1/2 teaspoon baking soda 1/2 teaspoon salt freshly ground nutmeg

---

## Instructions

Preheat oven to 400Â° F. Soak oats in buttermilk for one hour. Add egg and beat well. Add brown sugar and mix; add cooled shortening and mix. Stir flour, baking powder, soda, and salt together and add to batter; mix. Fill muffin tins 2/3 full and sprinkle with freshly ground nutmeg. Bake for 15-20 minutes. Eat with a smile.


---

## About This Recipe

This recipe comes from Ann's grandmother, Ann "Teena" Jacobson who lived in Chippewa Falls, Wisconsin. Teena's original hand-written recipe includes the injunction to eat them with a smile. These muffins are delicious plain or with butter and honey, and they are great for breakfast, brunch, or an all-purpose snack. Ann baked them several times for the Grant Program staff in the early 401 Wilshire days (ca. 1988-1991) when her colleagues would devour them. One colleague in particular was often inspired by these muffins to rail against the many injustices suffered by the homemade muffin in the face of the general population's declining standards for muffins, as influenced by the ubiquitous Starbuck's- and Souplantation-style muffins.
