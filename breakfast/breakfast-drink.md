# Breakfast Drink

**Contributor:** Dora Breece

**Getty Entity:** Facilities

**Department:** Administration

---

## Servings & Ingredients

Serves Variable 1 jar Trader Joe's juice (any flavor) 1 container vanilla rice milk (or soy milk in chocolate, carob or strawberry) nonfat powdered milk crushed ice Sugar Twin

---

## Instructions

Fill blender with 1/2 juice and 1/2 vanilla rice milk, add some nonfat powdered milk. Blend with (optional) crushed ice, and add (optional) Sugar Twin.
