# Egg in a Cup: an Egg-cellent Breakfast

**Contributor:** Christopher Lula

**Getty Entity:** Museum

**Department:** Visitor Services

---

## Servings & Ingredients

Serves 1 2 extra large eggs 2 slices of bread (any variety) butter or margarine salt and pepper

---

## Instructions

Fill medium saucepan or pot with water and gently drop in both eggs. Cook over high heat, bringing contents to a boil. When water begins to come to a full boil, continue to cook eggs for 4-5 minutes until soft-boiled. Drain water from saucepan and run cold water over eggs until they cool. Peel both eggs, place them in a cup and mash with a fork or cut into small pieces with a knife. Lightly toast and butter two pieces of bread. Cut each slice into small pieces or quarters. With fork, mash toast with eggs in the cup. Season with salt and pepper to taste and add extra butter or margarine if desired.


---

## About This Recipe

This easy recipe was taught to Christopher by his mother and is no doubt a British tradition. Although "Egg in the Cup" is one name used for this dish, it has other apellations originating in the U.K. However, for bachelors and others like Christopher, this quick and delicious breakfast item always satisfies, and the name says it all!
