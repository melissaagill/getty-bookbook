# Crunchy Granola

**Contributor:** Deborah Gribbon

**Getty Entity:** Museum

**Department:** Administration

---

## Servings & Ingredients

1/4 cup butter or margarine 1/4 cup sesame oil 1/4 cup brown sugar (or to taste) 1/2 teaspoon salt 1/2 cup water 1/2 teaspoon vanilla 4 cups uncooked old-fashioned oatmeal 1/2 cup wheat germ 1/2 cup shredded coconut 2 tablespoons sesame seeds 1/2 cup mixed nuts, chopped

---

## Instructions

Using a large pot and a wooden spoon, stir the butter or margarine, sesame oil, brown sugar, salt, water, and vanilla over low heat until melted. Remove the pot from stove and add all other ingredients. Mix well and spread out evenly onto a cookie sheet. Bake about 2 hours at 250Â° F. Stir lightly with a fork every 20 minutes or so. Store in a tight container.
