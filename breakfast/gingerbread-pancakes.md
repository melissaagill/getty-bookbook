# Gingerbread Pancakes

**Contributor:** Richard Lindstrom

**Getty Entity:** Research Institute

**Department:** Collection Development

---

## Servings & Ingredients

Serves 2 1 cup all-purpose flour 1 1/2 teaspoons baking powder 1/2 teaspoon ground cinnamon 1/2 teaspoon ground ginger 1 dash ground cloves 1/2 cup skim milk 1 tablespoon cooking oil 2 tablespoons molasses 1 extra large egg applesauce (optional)

---

## Instructions

Mix dry ingredients together in a large bowl. In a separate medium-sized bowl, beat egg until completely blended and mix in wet ingredients. Mix blended wet ingredients into dry ingredients only until batter is smooth and free of lumps. Cook on a hot griddle. Make pancakes small (silver dollar size), they fluff up thick. Serve with applesauce.


---

## About This Recipe

Richard Lindstrom is an archaeologist who could have really used some hot gingerbread pancakes to warm his bones during his last Siberian winter in Russia. Alas, he had to make do with a jar of preserved cauliflower.
