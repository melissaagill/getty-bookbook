# Curt's French Toast

**Contributor:** Curt Williams

**Getty Entity:** Trust

**Department:** Operations & Planning, Director

---

## Servings & Ingredients

Serves 4-6 1 8-oz. package cream cheese, softened strawberry jam 1 loaf French bread, sliced in half horizontally 4 extra large eggs 1/2 teaspoon salt 2 tablespoons sugar 1/2 cup milk butter or margarine

---

## Instructions

Prepare the evening before: mix softened cream cheese with just enough jam to color light pink. Spread on bread halves and put two halves back together. Wrap loaf and refrigerate overnight. In the morning: beat eggs, salt, sugar and milk together. Remove bread from refrigerator and slice with a bread knife approximately 1 inch thick. Preheat an electric griddle to 350Â° F and lightly grease with butter or margarine. Dip bread slices in egg mixture and fry until golden brown on both sides. Serve with fresh strawberries, a dusting of powered sugar, and Canadian bacon on the side. You may use any fresh fruit -- peaches, raspberries, etc. Just change the flavor of the jam you use in the cream cheese mixture.
